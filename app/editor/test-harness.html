State v. Halvard — Suppression Exercise

You are an attorney representing Devon Halvard, who was arrested on suspicion of driving while impaired after a late-night traffic stop on an icy county road. The State's evidence rests on a breath test administered at the station and the arresting officer's account of the stop.

Halden Regional District v. Devon Halvard (DWI · Suppression)

Written objectives. Diagnose the lawfulness of the stop, marshal the facts that bear on reasonable articulable suspicion, and produce a suppression memorandum that states the controlling standard before applying it to the icy-road facts.

The client wants the breath-test result kept out and is worried about losing the delivery-route license that supports the family. He has no prior record and coaches a youth hockey team on weekends.

The officer's report describes the road as untreated and the stop as routine.

Counsel should weigh the timeline between the stop and the station test.
