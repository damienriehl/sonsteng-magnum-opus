# Exhibit m03.exh.002 — Intersection & Scene Diagram {#b:be942fa4}

*Description of the stipulated field diagram attached to the Elleston Police crash report. In the trial packet this is the load-bearing demonstrative exhibit; a scaled diagram is prepared from this description.* {#b:ce91e00f}

**Vantage:** Overhead (plan) view of the intersection of Elleston Road and Birchmont Avenue, Elleston, Marrick County. {#b:b974f836}

**Roadway geometry:** {#b:d74f3288}
- Elleston Road runs north–south and carries two northbound travel lanes (left and right) approaching the Birchmont Avenue signal. The approach is on a gentle downgrade toward the intersection. {#b:fc7fb5d0}
- Birchmont Avenue runs east–west and crosses at a signalized intersection. {#b:30dada99}
- A standard three-phase traffic signal governs the intersection; it was displaying red for northbound Elleston Road traffic at the time of the crash. {#b:3a5bbb2a}

**Vehicle and witness positions marked on the diagram:** {#b:7e37d4ea}
- **V1 (Petimeyer sedan):** stopped in the right northbound lane, second in line behind one other stopped vehicle, its front bumper short of the crosswalk. {#b:99e77240}
- **Lead vehicle:** stopped ahead of V1 in the right lane (uninvolved). {#b:70f25f94}
- **V2 (Ashcombe pickup):** in the right northbound lane behind V1, shown mid-slide with a short skid/slide path to the point of impact at the rear of V1. {#b:91e3e07f}
- **Witness (Quesada):** stopped in the left northbound lane, roughly one car length behind the front of V1. {#b:a832efc9}
- **Point of impact:** rear bumper/trunk of V1. {#b:4695ccab}
- **Rest positions:** V1 pushed forward a short distance; V2 in contact with the rear of V1. {#b:3d54cfe6}

**Annotations:** downgrade arrow on the Elleston Road approach; "ICE — untreated" notation across the northbound lanes; posted speed limit 30 mph marked on the approach. {#b:378a8ca4}

*Prepared from Officer Harmon's field diagram (Exhibit m03.exh.001).* {#b:67949b12}
