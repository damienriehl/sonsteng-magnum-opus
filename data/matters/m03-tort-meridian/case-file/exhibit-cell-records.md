| Time | Activity type | Direction | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

**Points in dispute reflected by this exhibit:**
- The 7:42 a.m. outgoing text was sent within approximately one minute of the estimated 7:43 a.m. time of impact.
- The carrier record shows that a message was sent; it does not indicate whether the message was typed by hand on the device or composed by hands-free voice dictation.
- Ashcombe contends the message was dictated hands-free through his vehicle's system and that he did not handle the phone. No independent record confirms or refutes that account.
