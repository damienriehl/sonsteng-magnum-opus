# Exhibit m03.exh.003 — Vehicle-Damage Photographs (Described) {#b:856b4519}

*Descriptions of the photographs in the file. Actual images are held in the matter file; the descriptions govern the record for teaching purposes.* {#b:f34dc2c6}

**Photograph 1 — Petimeyer sedan, rear three-quarter view.** Shows the rear of the four-door sedan with the trunk lid buckled upward, the rear bumper cover cracked and pushed in on the left side, and the license-plate area deformed. Taillight housing on the driver's side is broken. {#b:daa7594c}

**Photograph 2 — Petimeyer sedan, close rear.** Close view of the crushed bumper reinforcement and a gap between the bumper cover and the quarter panel. No intrusion into the passenger compartment; airbags not deployed. {#b:e346c403}

**Photograph 3 — Petimeyer sedan, interior.** Driver's seat and headrest shown in normal position; no visible interior damage. {#b:f75acb73}

**Photograph 4 — Ashcombe pickup, front view.** Full-size pickup with a scuff and minor dent to the front bumper and a cracked lower fascia; no other visible damage. The truck's ride height places its bumper higher than the sedan's bumper. {#b:85241267}

**Photograph 5 — Roadway surface.** Wide shot of the northbound approach on Elleston Road showing a glazed, reflective ice surface across both lanes and no visible sand or treatment. A downgrade toward the signal is visible. {#b:be6c93c9}

**Photograph 6 — Signal and intersection.** The Birchmont Avenue signal displaying red for the northbound approach, with both vehicles visible in their post-crash positions. {#b:f41b21d7}

*Photographs taken at the scene and shortly afterward by the responding officer and the plaintiff.* {#b:5be18912}
