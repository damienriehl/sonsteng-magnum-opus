# Exhibit m03.exh.007 — Wage-Loss Statement {#b:f8c14703}

*Summary of Lorraine Petimeyer's claimed lost earnings from missed and reduced bus-route work following the collision.* {#b:e3bf0470}

- **Employer:** Elleston area school-transportation service (part-time bus driver) {#b:853e30ae}
- **Regular schedule:** Split morning and afternoon routes, five days per week during the school year {#b:19f98623}

**Missed work:** {#b:fab103fc}
- Full shifts missed: approximately three weeks of morning-and-afternoon route days in the period following the February 7, 2025 collision, while symptoms were most acute and she was beginning treatment. {#b:c883407e}
- Reduced hours: a subsequent period of reduced hours, during which she declined or shortened afternoon shifts because of neck and shoulder pain aggravated by repeated over-the-shoulder checks and by helping secure the wheelchair ramp. {#b:dd57ec4f}

**Claimed lost wages:** approximately $3,200, based on her documented hourly rate and scheduled route hours during the affected period. {#b:93643264}

**Supporting materials in file:** route schedule, timesheets showing missed and shortened shifts, and pay records establishing the hourly rate. {#b:f6535cb0}
