Petitioner requests that the Court dissolve the parties' marriage and enter the orders set out below. {#b:6b4630ea}

1. **Residence.** Petitioner and Respondent have each been residents of the State of California for more than six months and of the County of Alameda for more than three months immediately preceding the filing of this Petition. {#b:18a2ac00}

2. **Marriage.** The parties were married on April 21, 2018, and separated on or about January 15, 2026. The period from marriage to separation is approximately seven years and nine months. {#b:06747876}

3. **Minor child.** There is one minor child of the marriage: {#b:ab59df32}
   - Noémie Lassiter, born August 14, 2019. {#b:c19b3700}

4. **Grounds.** This Petition is brought on the basis of irreconcilable differences that have caused the irremediable breakdown of the marriage. {#b:d838b9bc}

5. **Legal and physical custody.** Petitioner requests that the parties share **joint legal custody** of the minor child, that Petitioner be awarded **primary physical custody**, and that the Court order a parenting-time schedule that provides the child a predictable routine while affording Respondent regular and meaningful time consistent with the parties' work schedules. {#b:1811a4eb}

6. **Child and spousal support.** Petitioner requests that the Court order child support in accordance with law and reserve jurisdiction over spousal support for both parties. {#b:4a31ca6f}

7. **Property.** Petitioner requests that the Court: {#b:acdc54fe}
   - determine the parties' rights in the community and separate property, including the parties' interests in **Petite Marigny LLC** and the family residence on Chabot Terrace, Oakland; {#b:22c55eb7}
   - confirm to each party his or her separate property, if any, subject to proof of characterization and tracing; {#b:46a164cf}
   - divide the community estate; and {#b:f0bad98e}
   - order each party to complete and exchange the required declarations of disclosure. {#b:ae74f287}

8. **Attorney's fees and costs.** Petitioner requests an award of attorney's fees and costs as the Court finds just. {#b:78b0d761}

9. **Other.** Petitioner requests such other and further relief as the Court deems proper. {#b:710d6949}
