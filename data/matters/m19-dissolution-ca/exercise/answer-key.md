## 1. Model Case Analysis (keyed to fact anchors)

### 1.1 Posture
The firm represents **Camille Beaumont, petitioner** [role `m19.role.petitioner-counsel`]. Petition for Dissolution filed Feb. 27, 2026 in the Family Law Division, Superior Court, County of Alameda [m19.fact.052]. Marriage April 2018 (~8 years) [m19.fact.001]–[m19.fact.002]; one child, Noémie, age 6 [m19.fact.003]. California is a **community-property** state — property/debt acquired during marriage is presumptively community, divided equally at dissolution (Fam. Code §§ 760, 2550; separate property § 770) `[instructor: verify before teaching]`. Dissolution is no-fault; the leverage lives at the seams of the equal-division rule.

### 1.2 Issue A — Characterization, tracing, reimbursement (rubric c03.s01, 30 pts)
Andre's **$80,000 inheritance** (received 2016, pre-marriage, kept in a separate credit-union account) is **separate in origin** [m19.fact.016]. In 2019 he transferred it into the restaurant buildout — kitchen, bar, leasehold improvements [m19.fact.017]. Two doctrines:
- **Tracing / commingling.** The party asserting a separate interest **bears the burden of tracing** it into the community asset; commingling over years can defeat the claim (See v. See, 64 Cal.2d 778 (1966)) `[instructor: verify before teaching]`. The file's gaps are deliberate and fatal-if-unmet: a single 2016 statement, a 2019 wire of only **$52,000** (not $80,000), no continuous trail [instructor notes; m19.fact.019]. Andre resists producing clean records [m19.fact.019, persona concealed].
- **Reimbursement.** Even if traced, the remedy is not ownership but a **statutory reimbursement right** for separate contributions to the acquisition of community property (the Fam. Code § 2640 concept — reimbursement without interest/appreciation, limited to documented contributions) `[instructor: verify before teaching]`. A model answer: Andre is likely limited to reimbursement of what he can **actually trace** (~$52k on this record), not a pre-division ownership carve-out, and the business itself remains community.

### 1.3 Issue B — Business valuation & goodwill (rubric c03.s02, 30 pts)
Petite Marigny is a two-member LLC opened during the marriage [m19.fact.013], [m19.fact.015] — a community asset. The crux is **enterprise vs. personal (professional) goodwill**: California treats the goodwill of an owner-operated/professional business as divisible community property to the extent it is *enterprise* goodwill (transferable — license, lease, name, systems, clientele, reviews), but personal goodwill tied to a specific individual is not freely transferable (In re Marriage of Golden and the goodwill line) `[instructor: verify before teaching]`. Andre's "it's my hands and my name" [m19.fact.021] is the personal-goodwill argument; Camille's "license, equipment, lease, name, reviews, book of business" [m19.fact.020], [m19.fact.044] is the enterprise-value argument, corroborated by Tessier [m19.fact.020, persona] ("a good chef could keep it going"). Income swings ~$40k–$90k, best year 2024 [m19.fact.022]. The jointly retained analyst Ferrante issued a **preliminary** report flagging a cash-deposit irregularity [m19.fact.024], [m19.fact.054] — a model answer identifies the discovery/expert work still needed (valuation date, normalization of owner compensation, the cash irregularity) and treats the number as unsettled.

### 1.4 Issue C — Custody & the parenting schedule (priority)
Both parents are fit; the fight is the **schedule**, not fitness [m19.fact.032]. Custody turns on the **best-interests standard** (Fam. Code §§ 3011, 3020, 3040), with Family Court Services mediation `[instructor: verify before teaching]`. Camille's **primary-caretaker history** (mornings, drop-offs, pediatrician, bedtime) [m19.fact.028] and Andre's genuinely constrained night/weekend hours [m19.fact.011], [m19.fact.031] support her request for primary physical custody + a predictable schedule + joint legal custody [m19.fact.043], and keeping the Chabot Terrace home so Noémie doesn't change schools [m19.fact.025]–[m19.fact.026]. Andre seeks ~equal time and says he can restructure hours [m19.fact.033], [m19.fact.045]. The **June 2025 late-pickup / drinking** incident [m19.fact.037]–[m19.fact.040] bears on unsupervised overnights and must be handled **proportionately** — alcohol use is addressed as a targeted safety concern (e.g., structured/stepped-up parenting time, sobriety conditions), **not an automatic disqualifier**, and not as a weapon `[instructor: verify before teaching]`.

### 1.5 The client's own exposure — the moved funds (candor)
Around the separation, Camille quietly moved **~$18,000** from the joint account and restaurant cash deposits into a personal account Andre doesn't know about [m19.fact.048]. This is the matter's ethical hinge. Each spouse owes a **fiduciary duty of full and accurate disclosure of community assets** (Fam. Code §§ 721, 1100(e), and the § 2100-series declaration-of-disclosure requirements) `[instructor: verify before teaching]`. A model answer treats the moved funds as a **disclosure problem to be cured** — the money must be disclosed in the declarations of disclosure and can be characterized as an interim community advance/withdrawal, not hidden — and counsels Camille that concealment risks sanctions and credibility, and worse for custody, than candid disclosure of a defensive but reversible act.

### 1.6 Spousal support / other property
Roughly comparable owner draws (~$62k Camille [m19.fact.007]) and an 8-year marriage frame temporary and long-term support questions (Fam. Code §§ 3600, 4320) `[instructor: verify before teaching]`. The Chabot Terrace home is agreed community (only who-keeps-it is disputed) [m19.fact.026]; Andre's premarital retirement account has a disputed, undocumented separate portion [m19.fact.027].

## 4. Grading Guidance

- **The interview is the case.** The two most consequential facts — the drinking/pickup and the moved funds — will not surface unless earned. Penalize the student who bulldozes the interview and drafts on an incomplete record; reward the one who slows down and earns them.
- **Disclosure, not concealment, is the ethical answer.** The single highest-stakes judgment is that the ~$18k [m19.fact.048] is a **disclosure problem to cure**, not a secret to keep. A student who counsels concealment (or who never confronts the duty) fails the ethical core regardless of polish. This is the c02.s01 / c04.s02 hinge.
- **Reimbursement ≠ ownership.** Reward students who see that even a traced separate contribution yields a § 2640 reimbursement right (of what's documented, ~$52k here), not a pre-division ownership carve-out — and that the tracing burden and gaps cut against Andre.
- **Goodwill split drives the number.** Credit the enterprise-vs.-personal goodwill analysis and the identification of the discovery/expert work still needed; do not credit a student who accepts Andre's "worth nothing without me" or Ferrante's preliminary number at face value.
- **Proportionate on alcohol.** The drinking is a targeted overnight-safety issue, not an automatic disqualifier and not a weapon. Reward students who frame it proportionately and keep the child central.
- **Rule 4.2 bright line.** Any direct contact with Andre is a hard miss on c04.s01. Credit routing through Vasquez / discovery.
- **Two-axis debrief** per `docs/research/interview-pedagogy.md`: Axis A fact coverage (+ Rule 4.2 flag) and Axis B relational experience rated in character (this distressed client rates rapport and being genuinely heard highly).
