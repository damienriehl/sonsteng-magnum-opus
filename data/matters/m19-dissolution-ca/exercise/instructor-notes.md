## What the exercise is really testing

This is a community-property dissolution built around a rapport-gated client interview, a separate-property tracing fight, a chef-driven business valuation, and a professional-responsibility double: Rule 4.2 no-contact plus the client's own fiduciary duty of disclosure. The strongest students will (a) earn the two withheld facts (Andre's drinking / the June pickup, and the funds Camille moved) through empathy rather than pressure, (b) frame the estate correctly under the community-property presumption, and (c) spot that the client's moved funds are a disclosure problem to be cured, not a secret to be kept.

## Teaching points and where to send research

- **Community property and equal division.** California is a community-property state; property and debt acquired during marriage are presumptively community, divided equally. Start students in the California Family Code, Divisions 6 and 7 (see `ca.json`, "Dissolution and community property"). The home and the LLC acquired during the marriage are the core community estate.
- **Separate-property characterization, tracing, and reimbursement.** Andre's $80,000 inheritance (received 2016, pre-marriage) is separate in origin, but the party asserting a separate interest bears the burden of tracing, and commingling can defeat it. The file's gaps are deliberate: a single 2016 statement, a 2019 wire of only $52,000, no continuous trail. Send students to research separate-property tracing and the statutory reimbursement right for separate contributions to community property (the general Fam. Code § 2640 concept — instructor-verify current text against leginfo.legislature.ca.gov).
- **Business valuation / goodwill.** The enterprise-vs-personal goodwill split is the crux. Research how California treats goodwill of a professional or owner-operated business on dissolution and appropriate valuation dates. The analyst's report is intentionally preliminary and flags a cash-deposit irregularity.
- **Custody / best interests.** Legal vs. physical custody under the best-interests standard, Family Court Services mediation, and how a parent's alcohol use is addressed proportionately (not as automatic disqualification). The June 2025 pickup is the concrete hook.
- **Professional responsibility (two rules).** (1) Andre is represented by Corinne Vasquez; direct contact implicates Cal. R. Prof. Conduct 4.2 — route through counsel. (2) The funds Camille moved implicate each spouse's fiduciary duty of full disclosure of community assets (Fam. Code §§ 721, 1100, 2100-series concepts — instructor-verify). Counsel the client toward disclosure; do not help conceal.
