**STATE OF MERIDIAN**
**DISTRICT COURT — PROBATE DIVISION**

*In re the Estate of Mabel R. Thornquist, Deceased.* {#b:213ced43}

**OBJECTION AND PETITION TO CONTEST WILL** {#b:fa8b5573}

Ronald Thornquist, son of the decedent and an heir, objects to the will admitted to probate on August 11, 2025, and petitions to set it aside. In support, he states: {#b:1fcbd797}

1. The decedent, Mabel R. Thornquist, died on June 22, 2025, a resident of Sable County. {#b:57ccd806}

2. The decedent's son, Ronald Thornquist, and daughter, Eleanor Thornquist-Reddick, are her only children and heirs. {#b:74d28b60}

3. The instrument admitted to probate is dated October 18, 2024. It leaves the decedent's Loon Rush Lake home and the residue of the estate to Eleanor Thornquist-Reddick, names her personal representative, and leaves the contestant a cash bequest of $25,000. {#b:cf51f7d9}

4. The decedent's earlier will, dated May 12, 2015, left her estate to her two children in equal shares. {#b:ee6fbdc2}

5. **First ground — lack of testamentary capacity.** The contestant alleges that at the time she signed the 2024 will, the decedent did not understand the nature of her property, the natural objects of her bounty, or the effect of the instrument, by reason of her cognitive decline. {#b:e006b154}

6. **Second ground — undue influence.** The contestant alleges that Eleanor Thornquist-Reddick, who lived with and cared for the decedent, held her financial power of attorney, controlled access to her, and stood to benefit, procured the 2024 will through undue influence. {#b:57a38f32}

7. The contestant asks the court to set aside the 2024 will and to admit the 2015 will, or to distribute the estate as if the decedent left no valid will. {#b:27815e16}

WHEREFORE, the contestant prays for relief as stated. {#b:e5df0326}

*Filed September 15, 2025.*
Ronald Thornquist, by counsel, Placidia Ohnstad, Esq. {#b:3e0eb0d1}
