# Witness Statement — Lorna Sundberg {#b:ec2e20ca}

**Witness:** Lorna Sundberg
**Role:** Home health aide for Mabel Thornquist
**Date of statement:** October 6, 2025
**Taken at:** Brightwater, Sable County, Meridian {#b:29e62804}

---

My name is Lorna Sundberg. I am thirty-four and I work as a home health aide. I was assigned to Mabel Thornquist starting in the spring of 2024, and I worked weekday afternoons at the lake house through the rest of that year and into 2025, until close to the end. {#b:da49b586}

My job was to help Mabel with her afternoons — a light meal, her medications, keeping her comfortable and company. I was fond of her. She liked to sit by the window and watch the lake. {#b:bff47f4a}

If you ask me how she was, the honest answer is that it went up and down. Some afternoons Mabel was clear and funny and knew exactly what was going on. Other afternoons she was foggy — she'd ask me the same question a few times, or she wouldn't be sure what day it was. Her best time was usually the morning; by late afternoon she'd tire and get more confused. I wasn't there in the mornings, so I only saw her at her more tired time of day. {#b:9cd7ee9d}

Eleanor was there constantly. I want to be fair about that. Whatever anyone says, I never saw Eleanor be rough or unkind to her mother. She was patient with her. She did the hard, unglamorous work of taking care of someone who is slipping — the meals, the medicine, the cleaning up. I saw that with my own eyes, day after day. {#b:3d4b1556}

There is one thing I have gone back and forth about mentioning, because I don't want it taken the wrong way. One afternoon, Eleanor was frustrated — tired, I think — and I heard her say to Mabel something like, "Ronald doesn't care about you." Mabel just nodded. I don't know what to make of it. People say things when they're worn out. But I heard it, and I don't want to pretend I didn't. {#b:205d67f3}

I'll also say I'm uneasy being in the middle of this. Eleanor is the one who arranged for me to be paid, and I know someone could say that makes me biased toward her. That's part of why I've tried to just say what I actually saw and not more. {#b:95461c5e}

I was not at the lawyer's office the day Mabel signed her will. I have no idea how she was that day. I can only speak to the afternoons I was there. {#b:cd8dba9a}

*/s/ Lorna Sundberg* {#b:116b36c7}
