## Job Description — Principal Engineer, Gait Access Platform {#b:eb5a72ab}

- Architect and lead development of a novel gait-recognition model for physical access control. {#b:def5e608}
- Target deployment: enrolled-user recognition at doorways and secure entries (small, cooperative, opt-in populations). {#b:6b7147a1}
- Customers: building-security integrators, logistics and warehouse operators, facilities managers. {#b:555117c4}
- **Out of scope:** face recognition; large-database identity search; law-enforcement or surveillance watch-list matching. {#b:cf2da6c2}
