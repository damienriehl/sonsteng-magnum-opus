## 1. Opposing counsel to Karsgaard — September 18, 2024, 4:31 p.m.

> **From:** Odalys Prentice
> **To:** Delphine Karsgaard
> **Subject:** Dieterle v. Larkspur Market — service

> Delphine — We represent Larkspur Market Holdings. We have never been served with the summons and complaint in this matter, and the time to complete service has now passed. Unless I hear otherwise with proof of timely service, we intend to move to dismiss. Please advise.
