## Notice of Motion and Motion {#b:03b455db}

**Dieterle v. Larkspur Market Holdings, LLC — District Court** {#b:b522fe44}

Plaintiff Marguerite Dieterle, by counsel Delphine A. Karsgaard, moves the Court for an order vacating the dismissal entered October 2, 2024, and reinstating this action, on the ground of excusable neglect. This motion is supported by the attached Affidavit of Delphine A. Karsgaard. {#b:e9d3fcfc}

Dated: December 10, 2024
Delphine A. Karsgaard, Attorney for Plaintiff {#b:f3d0f071}

---

## Affidavit of Delphine A. Karsgaard {#b:ec0ea075}

STATE OF MINNESOTA
COUNTY OF [County] {#b:a13c97cf}

Delphine A. Karsgaard, being duly sworn, deposes and states: {#b:7b8a6fe6}

1. I am the attorney for plaintiff Marguerite Dieterle in this action and make this affidavit in support of the motion to vacate the dismissal. {#b:14d9afae}

2. This matter was filed in May 2024. The deadline to complete service on the defendant was in August 2024. {#b:91eafdf9}

3. My office's litigation deadlines had been maintained by my paralegal, who left the firm at the end of June 2024. In the transition, the service deadline for this matter was not carried forward, and service was not completed. {#b:dc4d19e3}

4. **I first became aware of the failure of service on November 26, 2024**, when my client contacted my office to inquire about the status of the matter. {#b:8cae25ed}

5. Upon learning of the failure, I promptly investigated, confirmed the dismissal, and prepared this motion. {#b:f9032729}

6. The failure was the result of an inadvertent calendaring lapse during a staffing transition, not a deliberate disregard of the matter. Plaintiff has a meritorious claim and should not lose it because of this office error. Defendant will suffer no material prejudice from reinstatement. {#b:3a9befa4}

WHEREFORE, plaintiff respectfully requests that the dismissal be vacated and the action reinstated. {#b:f32c143e}

_______________________________
Delphine A. Karsgaard {#b:4773d420}

Subscribed and sworn before me this 10th day of December, 2024. {#b:564e5d74}

*[Note for the file: paragraph 4's "first became aware … November 26, 2024" is the statement the Director alleges was false, in light of the September 18 email and the October 2 order. See the correspondence and docketing exhibits.]* {#b:7b683878}
