# Instructor Notes — m11 Employment Arbitration (INSTRUCTOR ONLY)

*Not for distribution to students. Teaching notes only — no answer key. Citations below are navigational starting points, instructor-verified at publish against the official Illinois sources in `data/jurisdictions/real/il.json`; confirm currency before teaching.*

## What this exercise is drilling

- The pivot from court to **private arbitration**: the parties' agreement supplies procedure; the arbitrator decides fact and law; a court's role is limited to compelling arbitration and confirming/vacating an award. Students should feel how the forum changes strategy (streamlined record, employer pays the neutral, class waiver).
- A **retaliation / wrongful-discharge** proof problem built on timing and pretext, with a documentary chronology that lets the causal story be assembled from exhibits.
- A live **Rule 4.2 no-contact** teaching moment: the director of operations (Pfeiffer) and HR partner (Croll) are the represented employer's people; students must route contact through opposing counsel or discovery, not interview Pfeiffer directly.

## Navigational research pointers (instructor-verified)

- **Arbitration enforceability / confirming or vacating an award:** Illinois Uniform Arbitration Act, 710 ILCS 5; the Federal Arbitration Act, 9 U.S.C. §§ 1–16, frames enforceability and the interstate-commerce question for a logistics employer. Note the FAA § 1 transportation-worker exemption is worth having students test against these facts.
- **Retaliatory discharge (Illinois common law)** and statutory retaliation protections; wage-and-hour retaliation and off-the-clock pay under the Illinois Wage Payment and Collection Act and the Illinois Minimum Wage Law; safety-complaint protection. Let students find and frame the elements themselves.
- **Professional conduct:** Ill. R. Prof. Conduct 4.2 (communication with a represented person). Discuss the managerial-employee scope for an organizational party.

## Chronology backbone (for grading the students' chronology)

- 2025-09 verbal complaint to Pfeiffer → 2025-11-05 fire-exit photo → 2025-11-10 written HR complaint → 2025-11-12 Croll forwards to Pfeiffer → 2025-11-18 state hotline → 2025-12-05 PIP → 2026-01-08 RIF memo → 2026-01-09 termination → within 6 weeks, reposting.
- The November 12 internal forward (Exhibit m11.exh.004) is the knowledge linchpin against the employer's ignorance defense.

## Facilitation cautions

- The client persona holds three rapport-gated facts (blacklisting fear, private notebook, the August timecard edit) and one concealed fact (the October job application). Coach students that pressure never unlocks these — only the emotional preconditions do.
- Keep student drafts facts-only; the legal research is the point.
