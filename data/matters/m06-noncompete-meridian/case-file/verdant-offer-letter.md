# Exhibit 005 — Verdant Medical Systems Offer Letter and Job Description

**VERDANT MEDICAL SYSTEMS, LLC**
Elleston, Marrick County, Meridian

**Date:** December 18, 2025
**To:** Adaeze Okwuosa
**Re:** Offer of Employment — Clinical Sales Specialist, Endoscopy & GI

Dear Ms. Okwuosa,

We are delighted to offer you the position of **Clinical Sales Specialist, Endoscopy & GI Division**, reporting to Curtis Bellweather, Regional Manager. Your assigned territory will be the northern and western counties of our Meridian region. Your anticipated start date is January 6, 2026.

**Position summary.** You will sell and clinically support Verdant's flexible endoscopy and gastrointestinal-suite product lines to hospitals and endoscopy centers in your territory. This is a distinct product category from orthopedic implants and trays.

**Compliance condition (Section 5).** Verdant requires all incoming sales personnel to certify that they will **not use, disclose, or bring to Verdant any confidential or proprietary information of a former employer**, including customer lists or pricing data. You may not solicit business using any such information. By countersigning, you certify your understanding of and agreement to this condition.

We ask that you review the attached job description and the compliance certification and return signed copies before your start date. We are excited to have you join the team.

Sincerely,
/s/ Curtis Bellweather, Regional Manager, Endoscopy & GI
