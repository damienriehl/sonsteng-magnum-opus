# Exhibit m17.exh.001 — Master Supply Agreement and Purchase Orders {#b:ddabb9f2}

**Parties:** Empire Provisions Corp. ("Buyer") and Ostervold Packing Co. ("Seller")
**Agreement date:** May 15, 2025
**Term:** 2025 packing season; six (6) truckload releases by individual Purchase Order {#b:64bc48f6}

---

## 1. Goods {#b:288ce412}

Seller shall pack and deliver Buyer's private-label specialty-food line, consisting of jarred sauces, packaged grains, and bottled olive-oil products, in the quantities and configurations stated on each Purchase Order. Each truckload release is priced at approximately $48,000, confirmed on the applicable Purchase Order. {#b:6b6de03c}

## 2. Product specifications {#b:5e9ef506}

Each unit delivered under this Agreement shall conform to the following specifications: {#b:afb70b07}

- **(a) Net weight.** The declared net weight on each unit label shall be accurate to the unit's actual filled weight within the tolerance stated on the approved artwork. {#b:f6b441a7}
- **(b) Allergen and ingredient statement.** Each unit label shall bear a complete and accurate allergen and ingredient statement that matches, word for word, the artwork proof approved in writing by Buyer before the run. {#b:5355a96f}
- **(c) Date coding.** Each unit shall bear a printed "best by" date code in the format and location shown on the approved artwork. {#b:54807de4}
- **(d) Minimum remaining shelf life.** Each case shall arrive at Buyer's dock with **not less than nine (9) months** remaining before its printed "best by" date. {#b:cc317310}
- **(e) Packaging integrity.** Cases and units shall arrive intact, sealed, and free of crushing, leakage, or seal failure. {#b:cdde0047}

## 3. Inspection and rejection {#b:0d77602b}

Buyer may inspect goods on receipt and may reject any goods that fail to conform to the specifications in Section 2. Buyer shall act **promptly** in inspecting and in giving any notice of rejection. Rejected goods shall be held for Seller's account and disposition. *(No fixed number of days for rejection is stated in this Agreement.)* {#b:e9129161}

## 4. Cure {#b:3c52c75e}

If Buyer rejects a delivery as nonconforming, Seller may cure the nonconformity within a reasonable time by picking up, correcting, replacing, or redelivering conforming goods, at Seller's expense. {#b:8138e8f6}

## 5. Payment {#b:ca8461f5}

Buyer shall pay each conforming release within thirty (30) days of delivery and acceptance. {#b:8b1ae014}
