## D. Competing Positions {#b:ff532723}

- **Petitioner's position:** The business is a marital asset. When the truck, trailer, tool inventory, account balance, and goodwill are counted, its fair value is materially higher than Respondent concedes. {#b:49803f95}
- **Respondent's position:** The business is inseparable from his personal labor. Absent him, it is "a used truck and a pile of tools." He resists any goodwill figure and characterizes the enterprise as worth little as a going concern without him. {#b:a2da7f43}
