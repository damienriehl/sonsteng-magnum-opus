The children's surname is Aldergrove [m09.fact.005]. {#b:8fc1ff34}

Renata Sitko is forty-one and works as a nurse-manager — the charge nurse responsible for a medical-surgical unit — at a Norsholm hospital, where she has been employed for nine years [m09.fact.006]. Her position is salaried at approximately ninety-six thousand dollars a year and carries health, dental, and retirement benefits that cover the whole family [m09.fact.007]. Her schedule rotates: she works a mix of day and evening shifts and is on call roughly one weekend in three [m09.fact.008]. {#b:557c9de7}

Harlan Aldergrove is forty-three and owns Aldergrove Remodeling & Restoration LLC, a home-remodeling contracting business he started in 2016 [m09.fact.009]. His income is seasonal and uneven — strong from spring through fall, thin in the winter — and his tax returns show net earnings ranging from about fifty-eight thousand to seventy-two thousand dollars over the last three years [m09.fact.011]. {#b:182b20e5}

## How the marriage broke down {#b:cafd6203}

By 2024 the marriage was under strain over money and the imbalance of household labor [m09.fact.028]. The couple attended six sessions of marriage counseling in the fall of 2024, which did not resolve their conflicts [m09.fact.029]. Tension deepened over the winter of 2024–2025 when the business had a slow season and the couple argued repeatedly about finances [m09.fact.030]. {#b:23be5316}

During one such argument in February 2025, in the evening while both children were home, Harlan lost his temper, shouted, punched a hole in the hallway drywall, and briefly stood in the doorway blocking Renata from leaving the room before he stepped aside [m09.fact.031]. No one was struck and no police were called, but the incident frightened Renata and the children heard the shouting from upstairs [m09.fact.032]. Harlan later patched and repainted the drywall and apologized; he describes it as the worst moment of his life and says it never happened before or since [m09.fact.033]. {#b:8b936032}

Renata told Harlan she wanted a divorce in October 2025 [m09.fact.034]. Harlan moved out of the house in November 2025 and rented a two-bedroom apartment about ten minutes away, where the children have their own room [m09.fact.035]. Since the separation the parties have followed an informal week-on/week-off-ish schedule that neither is satisfied with, and disputes over pickups and holidays have grown frequent [m09.fact.036]. {#b:5961bbeb}
