## 1. Model Case Analysis (keyed to fact anchors)

This matter has **no villain**. Two decent, exhausted parents each fear losing time with children both of them love [m09.fact.017], [m09.fact.018], tangled in a jointly built house [m09.fact.019], a hard-to-value one-person business [m09.fact.009], and a real income-and-schedule disparity [m09.fact.007], [m09.fact.011]. The A-grade analysis resists the pull to pick a hero and instead runs four disciplined tracks — property classification/division, maintenance, best-interests custody, and settlement posture — while being candid that the custody and maintenance questions are **closely balanced, not one-sided** [m09.fact.054].

### Track 1 — Property: classification then equitable division

Meridian is a **no-fault, equitable-distribution** jurisdiction — marital property is divided justly and equitably, which is not always equally `[instructor: research target — verify Meridian authority before teaching]`. The A-answer separates *classification* (is it marital?) from *valuation* from *division*, and does not blur them.

**The marital home** [m09.fact.019], [m09.fact.020].
- Classification is easy: bought in 2014 during the marriage, titled jointly [m09.fact.019] — marital property.
- Valuation is largely settled: a 2026 joint appraisal opines **$385,000**, mortgage **≈$210,412**, marital equity **≈$174,588** [m09.fact.020].
- The live fight is Harlan's **sweat-equity** argument: he personally remodeled the kitchen, finished the basement, and re-sided the house, and believes his labor added value a "mechanical appraisal understates" [m09.fact.021]. A strong answer meets this head-on: the appraisal already reflects market value **as improved** and notes finish quality "above typical for the neighborhood" — the appraiser did not discount for the labor's *source* [m09.fact.020]. Improvements to a **marital** home performed with **marital-period labor** are generally already captured in the marital equity; the sweat-equity claim is real rhetoric but weak as a legal offset because the value is already in the number the parties jointly commissioned. The honest concession: a court has equitable discretion to credit disproportionate contribution, so treat this as a *negotiation* chip, not a doctrinal winner.
- Renata's plan — take the home subject to the mortgage with an **equity offset** to Harlan so the kids stay at Verdant Shore Elementary [m09.fact.038], [m09.fact.020] — is sound and child-centered. A-answers flag the financing reality: a refinance pre-qualification to remove Harlan from the loan is pending [m09.fact.020 / marital-home-records], and the buyout (~$87k of his half-equity) is the mechanism that funds his exit.

**The business — Aldergrove Remodeling & Restoration LLC** [m09.fact.009], [m09.fact.022]–[m09.fact.027]. This is the analytical heart of the property fight and the c03.s02 discriminator.
- Classification is again easy: formed 2016, during the marriage [m09.fact.009] — a marital asset [m09.fact.024], as the petition already pleads [petition ¶8].
- The fight is **valuation**, and the sophisticated move is to split the value into **tangible assets** vs. **goodwill**, then split goodwill into **enterprise goodwill** (transferable, generally divisible) vs. **personal/professional goodwill** (inseparable from the individual, in many jurisdictions **not** divisible) `[instructor: research target — verify Meridian authority before teaching]`.
  - *Tangible, indisputably marital and divisible:* 2019 work truck (~$28,000), enclosed trailer (~$7,500), tools/equipment (~$22,000–$30,000), operating account (~$6,100) — roughly **$63,600–$71,600** before goodwill [business-financial-summary §B].
  - *Goodwill, disputed:* the known local name, website, repeat-customer/referral pipeline, and a **backlog of jobs booked months ahead** [m09.fact.023], [business-financial-summary §C].
- **Harlan's strongest theory** (know it to beat it): the enterprise is "just me and my hands"; nobody can force him to keep swinging a hammer, so as a going concern without him it is "a used truck and a pile of tools" [m09.fact.025]. This is not frivolous — for a solo contractor much of the goodwill genuinely **is personal**, which cuts against a large goodwill add-on. A top student concedes the personal-goodwill point to *win* the tangible-asset and enterprise-goodwill points rather than overclaiming.
- **Renata's strongest theory:** the "just me" story is not honest once you count the truck, trailer, tool inventory, name, website, and the **booked pipeline** he is concealing [m09.fact.024], [m09.fact.039], [m09.fact.023]. Even accepting personal goodwill, the tangible assets alone are a six-figure-adjacent marital pool that must be valued and offset.
- **The cash-income wildcard** [m09.fact.027]. Renata suspects but cannot prove undisclosed cash "side jobs"; she never saw the books [m09.fact.027]. The accountant **can neither confirm nor rule it out** and says resolving it requires a forensic engagement not yet undertaken [m09.fact.027], [witness-statement-kilbane], [business-financial-summary §E]. The A-answer treats this correctly as a **discovery problem, not a proven fact**: it drives (a) the choice of a business-valuation expert, (b) a forensic-accounting request, and (c) its double relevance — cash receipts inflate both **business value** and **Harlan's true income** for maintenance and support. A student who asserts the cash as established fact has over-read the record.

### Track 2 — Spousal maintenance: the double-edged sword

Harlan intends to seek maintenance to offset the income disparity and his reinvestment in the family and house [m09.fact.041]. The A-answer counsels the **need vs. ability-to-pay** structure honestly and refuses to tell Renata what she wants to hear.
- **The gap is real but moderate.** Renata: ~$96,000 salaried with full family benefits [m09.fact.007]. Harlan: net ~$58,300–$71,800, seasonal, self-purchased individual coverage, no retirement [m09.fact.011], [income-benefits-summary]. The delta is roughly **$25k–$38k**, and Harlan is a *working business owner with earning capacity*, not a displaced homemaker — factors that typically point to **modest and/or time-limited (rehabilitative)** maintenance rather than a large permanent award over a ~14-year marriage [m09.fact.002] `[instructor: research target — verify Meridian authority before teaching]`.
- **The honest client message** (the teaching point the packet foregrounds): the *same fact* — her higher, steadier income — is her **strength on stability** (Track 3 custody) and her **exposure on maintenance** [m09.fact.054], [considerations §"double-edged income disparity"]. It cuts both ways. Counsel who lets her believe maintenance is off the table has malpracticed the counseling.
- **Interactions matter.** Maintenance does not stand alone: it interacts with (a) the **property award** — a larger equity buyout to Harlan reduces his need; (b) **child support** — if Renata holds primary physical custody, support runs from the higher earner toward the children but is offset by parenting-time and by Harlan's own obligation; and (c) the **undisclosed-cash question** — if Harlan's true income is higher than the books show [m09.fact.027], his *need* shrinks. A-answers plan discovery to develop these interactions before conceding a dollar.

### Track 3 — Best-interests custody: caretaker history vs. availability

Meridian resolves legal custody (decision-making) and physical custody (residence/routine care) under a **best-interests-of-the-child** standard driven by statutory factors, no-fault, with marital misconduct generally not itself controlling `[instructor: research target — verify Meridian authority before teaching]`, [substantive_info]. The GAL report is the spine of this analysis.
- **What the record proves for Renata:** she is the demonstrated **primary caretaker** — appointments, school, meals, homework, "the household calendar has always lived in Renata's head" [m09.fact.012], [witness-statement-ferreira], corroborated by the GAL ("the mother has historically carried the logistical parenting load") [gal-preliminary-report], the parenting journal [m09.fact.012 entries], and Yamada [witness-statement-yamada]. Her steady job and family benefits support **stability and continuity of school** [m09.fact.007], [m09.fact.038].
- **What the record proves for Harlan:** he is genuinely, daily involved — assistant-coaches Theo's hockey two seasons, bedtimes, weekend outings, a strong bond both children share [m09.fact.016], [m09.fact.017]; the GAL confirms "highly engaged... particularly through Theo's hockey," both homes "safe and appropriate," each child with an established bedroom in both homes [gal-preliminary-report], [m09.fact.035], [photographs-exhibit Photos 5–6]. His **off-season weekday availability** is the honest basis for his equal-time request [m09.fact.040], [income-benefits-summary].
- **The February 2025 incident** [m09.fact.031], [m09.fact.032], [m09.fact.033]. Handle it precisely: Harlan lost his temper, punched a hole in the hallway drywall, and briefly blocked Renata in a doorway before stepping aside; **no one was struck, no police were called**, the children were upstairs and heard the yelling, and he later patched/repainted and apologized [m09.fact.031], [m09.fact.032], [m09.fact.033]. The GAL flags it as **relevant to the parenting analysis but a single incident, not a present pattern**, warranting "continued attention, not" a pattern finding [gal-preliminary-report]; both parents corroborate no prior/subsequent physical aggression [m09.fact.033]. The A-answer neither weaponizes it into domestic-abuse-per-se nor waves it away — it is a genuine best-interests factor to be raised responsibly, especially against Harlan's equal-time claim.
- **The real obstacle is the schedule, not the label.** Junie repeatedly asks "whose night" it is; her teacher, Yamada, the journal, and the GAL all independently record her **schedule anxiety** [m09.fact.014-context], [parenting-journal Feb 10], [witness-statement-yamada], [gal-preliminary-report]. Hand-off mix-ups (the Jan 14 Lakeside pickup) upset the children [m09.fact.036], [parenting-journal Jan 14], [text-messages Jan 14], [witness-statement-yamada]. The GAL's throughline: the children benefit from a **predictable, written schedule regardless of the ultimate custody label** [gal-preliminary-report]. This is the single most important strategic insight in the matter (see Track 4).
- **Renata's two vulnerabilities**, to be counseled not concealed:
  - The **~two hours the children were left home alone** in late March 2025 (Theo ~11, Junie ~7) [m09.fact.043]. It is a single, explicable lapse driven by a staffing emergency, from which she drove home mid-shift when Theo called [m09.fact.043] — not neglect (Yamada: neither parent "neglectful," children always fed/clothed, a parent always answered) [witness-statement-yamada]. Counsel her that opposing counsel will try to frame it as "over-prioritizing work"; the answer is context and candor, not denial [m09.fact.044].
  - The **ongoing relationship with a coworker** that predated the divorce announcement [m09.fact.045], [m09.fact.046]. In a no-fault jurisdiction, marital misconduct is generally **not** a basis for custody or property, and the children have not met the person [m09.fact.046] `[instructor: research target — verify Meridian authority before teaching]`. It is relevant only if it affected the children or resources, which the record does not show. The sophisticated strategic question (raise in counseling and in the c04 confidentiality analysis) is whether counsel even *needs* it disclosed and whether it belongs in any served document — generally **no**.

### Track 4 — The A-grade conclusion and realistic settlement posture

Both parties genuinely want to spare the children a bruising trial and have signaled willingness to **settle property if the parenting schedule can be worked out** [m09.fact.053], [text-messages May 20], [parenting-journal May 13]. The A-grade recommendation therefore **prioritizes a durable, predictable parenting schedule and treats the property questions as tradeable** [m09.fact.053]:
- **Parenting:** joint **legal** custody (both are fit; both should decide) [m09.fact.037], [m09.fact.040]; primary **physical** residence with Renata for school-continuity, but a **generous, written, rotation-aware schedule** giving Harlan substantial and *predictable* time — built around her rotating day/evening shifts and one-in-three on-call weekends [m09.fact.008], [m09.fact.037] — rather than a rigid week-on/week-off that her shifts cannot support [text-messages Feb 2], [gal-preliminary-report]. Sell Renata off the *label* fight and onto *schedule durability* that ends Junie's "whose night" anxiety.
- **Property:** Renata keeps the home and **buys out** Harlan's ~$87k half-equity [m09.fact.020], [m09.fact.038]; the **tangible business assets** are valued and offset (conceding personal goodwill to win the tangibles), with a **forensic/valuation reservation** on the cash question [m09.fact.023]–[m09.fact.027]; property flexibility is the currency that buys the schedule.
- **Maintenance:** frame a **modest, time-limited** resolution (or an offsetting property/maintenance trade) that acknowledges Renata's real exposure without conceding a large permanent award [m09.fact.041], [m09.fact.054].
- **Temperature:** keep money talk away from the children (Harlan raised maintenance in front of Theo — a real friction point) [m09.fact.036], [parenting-journal Mar 7], [text-messages Mar 7], and channel Renata's understandable anger away from score-keeping that would frustrate the settlement both parents want [m09.fact.053], [witness-statement-ferreira].

An all-out trial is a worse expected outcome for *these* children than a well-structured settlement — and the record shows both parents already know it [m09.fact.053].

### Common errors (watch for these; build on instructor-notes)
1. **Racing to the property spreadsheet** — the packet's designed trap. A student who skips rapport drafts a competent-looking petition on an incomplete record and is ambushed later by the incident and the latchkey fact [considerations §"the interview is the case"].
2. **Pressuring or flattering the client** to unlock gated facts — by design it never works and it damages the relational axis; in the latchkey case an alarmed reaction re-conceals the fact.
3. **Contacting Harlan directly** — the signature ethics failure; automatic hit on c04.s01 [Mer. R. Prof. Conduct 4.2].
4. **Fighting the label instead of the schedule** — ignoring that Junie's anxiety and the hand-off conflicts turn on the *calendar*, not the words "sole" or "joint" [parenting-journal], [gal], [witness-statement-yamada].
5. **Over-claiming the business** — asserting a large goodwill figure without distinguishing personal from enterprise goodwill, or treating the suspected cash [m09.fact.027] as established rather than a discovery target.
6. **Weaponizing the February incident** — inflating a single, no-contact, remorseful episode into a domestic-abuse-per-se argument, or conversely waving it away; the GAL's calibrated "relevant, single incident, continued attention" framing is the model [gal].
7. **Letting sensitive facts bleed into served documents** — putting Renata's relationship [m09.fact.045] into a public filing; think carefully about Rule 1.6 and what must be served.
8. **Soft-pedaling maintenance** — telling Renata what she wants to hear about her exposure [m09.fact.041], [m09.fact.054]; a counseling failure even if the doctrine is otherwise right.
