## What the exercise is really testing

This is a three-variable transactional negotiation (access, severed minerals, seller's well reservation) wrapped around a hidden-deadline interview and a Rule 4.2 checkpoint. The strongest students will (a) refuse to treat the three problems as independent, (b) draw out the client's confidential solar option and March 31 deadline through rapport rather than interrogation, and (c) keep the client from contacting the represented seller.

## Teaching points and where to send research

- **Access / easement mismatch.** The recorded 1991 easement does not describe the traveled road (survey, Exhibit m14.exh.003). Send students to the Texas Property Code conveyance and recording provisions as a starting point (Tex. Prop. Code, Titles 2–3) and to research easements by express grant, easement by necessity, and easement by estoppel/implication for landlocked tracts. The practical cure menu: corrective/re-granted easement, a negotiated grant from Sandoval, or a suit to establish access.
- **Severed mineral estate / dominance.** The 1978 reservation (now Ocotillo Minerals, LP) is a severed estate. Students should research the surface/mineral dominance doctrine and the accommodation doctrine, and how a solar (surface-intensive) use interacts with a dominant mineral estate. The seller cannot convey what he does not own; the deed will except the minerals regardless.
- **Deed form / warranty.** General vs. special warranty deed — see the real-estate conventions in the jurisdiction file. Which form is negotiated matters for risk allocation around the easement and the mineral exception.
- **Seller's reservation.** A reserved surface easement for the well must be papered into contract and deed; negotiate size, location, duration, and whether it can be relocated out of the solar footprint. Consider Texas groundwater rules and whether the well can be replaced.
- **Professional responsibility.** Whitfield is represented by Faircloth. Communication with a represented adverse party without counsel's consent implicates Tex. Disciplinary R. Prof. Conduct 4.02. The client's prior direct texting with the seller (fact.041) is the live teachable moment — counsel must instruct the client to stop and route through Faircloth.

## Facilitation notes

- The client persona will not volunteer the solar option (fact.030), the real deadline (fact.031), or his true reservation price (fact.032, concealed) without earned rapport. Debrief students who bulldoze the interview.
- The seller persona is a represented adverse party; if students interview him directly from the buyer side, the Rule 4.2 teaching moment fires. Use it, do not skip it.
- Money/business layer: hourly engagement at the firm's blended senior rate; an unpaid balance is outstanding (aged) — useful for a business-of-law discussion on billing a mid-stream transactional matter.
