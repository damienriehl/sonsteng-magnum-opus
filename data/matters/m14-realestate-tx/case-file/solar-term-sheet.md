# Exhibit m14.exh.005 — Solar Developer Option & Ground-Lease Term Sheet (Confidential to Buyer) {#b:9bb3e64f}

**Nature:** Non-binding term sheet and signed option, from a solar developer to Kenji Nakamura, contingent on his acquisition of the Cimarron Tract.
**Dated:** October 30, 2025 {#b:54d377a0}

*Facts-only reproduction of the business terms. This document is confidential to the buyer's side; the seller has not seen it.* {#b:c8de6435}

## Option {#b:f01f1094}
The developer holds an **option** to lease approximately **180 acres** of the western pasture of the Cimarron Tract for a utility-scale solar array, exercisable once Nakamura closes on the tract. {#b:57510b8f}

## Key conditions (the reason timing matters) {#b:e23b64f4}
- **Buildable acreage.** Nakamura must deliver approximately 180 contiguous, unobstructed acres of the western flat — free of well sites, easements, or third-party surface uses that would sit inside the array footprint. {#b:1b880443}
- **Clear access.** Nakamura must deliver legally insurable, all-weather access adequate for construction equipment. {#b:9e119ba1}
- **Deadline.** These conditions must be satisfied by **March 31, 2026**, or the developer may terminate the option and walk. *(Note: this deadline is earlier than the April 30, 2026 real-estate closing.)* {#b:c3cc8f4a}

## Economics (summary) {#b:33627109}
- Annual ground rent escalating over a 30-year initial term, plus a one-time signing payment on exercise. {#b:c98d9f9a}
- Over its term, the lease's value to Nakamura substantially exceeds the $928,000 purchase price of the land. {#b:328a541c}

## Buyer's internal notes (privileged / confidential) {#b:b0bb9ab1}
- The Cimarron well and the mineral surface rights both threaten the "unobstructed 180 acres" condition; keeping the well where it is, or letting a mineral operator use the flat, could break the option. {#b:8ed09f1c}
- Because the lease is so valuable, losing the tract over the well or the easement would cost far more than paying to cure them — but the seller should not learn how valuable the solar deal is. {#b:0859bcf1}
