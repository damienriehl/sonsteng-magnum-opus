## Schedule B — Exceptions from Coverage {#b:4e2b76cc}
The policy will not insure against loss arising from the following, unless resolved before closing: {#b:08035021}

1. **Access easement of record — questionable.** That certain Access Easement recorded in 1991 in the real property records of Edwards County. The described corridor follows a fence-line course. The Company has received a survey (see Exhibit m14.exh.003) indicating the traveled road does not lie within the described corridor. **The Company is not prepared to insure access over this easement as described.** {#b:09f2eb2b}
2. **Reserved mineral estate.** All oil, gas, and other minerals reserved in a deed recorded in 1978, together with all rights incident thereto; said mineral estate now appearing of record in **Ocotillo Minerals, LP**. Seller does not own and cannot convey this estate. This exception will remain in the policy. {#b:6b878826}
3. **Rights of parties in possession / apparent pipeline.** An apparent buried water line running from the existing well toward the adjoining property to the northwest, source and authority not of record. {#b:08119092}
4. Standard survey, taxes, and easement/setback exceptions (routine). {#b:023a30b3}

## Schedule C — Requirements {#b:d16efabf}
To delete or modify the access exception (Exception 1), the Company requires **one** of the following: {#b:a56144fc}
- A recorded correction or re-grant of the access easement describing the road as actually located; **or** {#b:3b83fee4}
- A newly granted, surveyed access easement from the owner of the servient parcel; **or** {#b:8232ab50}
- A final judgment or other instrument establishing legal access over the road as located. {#b:afbdebf1}

## Escrow note {#b:ea14b113}
Earnest money of $15,000.00 is held by the Company. The Company stands ready to close upon receipt of resolved access, an agreed treatment of the mineral exception, and a signed deed in recordable form. {#b:15f1e735}
