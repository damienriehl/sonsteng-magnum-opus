# Exhibit m14.exh.001 — Farm and Ranch Earnest Money Contract (Excerpts) {#b:0d501d92}

**Property:** The Cimarron Tract — approximately 320 acres, Edwards County, Texas, being the land described in the attached Exhibit A legal description.
**Seller:** Garrett Whitfield
**Buyer:** Kenji Nakamura
**Effective Date:** November 14, 2025
**Escrow Agent:** Lone Mesa Title Company {#b:1886bb92}

*Facts-only reproduction of the operative business terms. Boilerplate omitted.* {#b:9d8c0e3e}

**1. Sales Price.** The total sales price is **$928,000.00**, payable in cash at closing, buyer to obtain third-party farm-and-ranch financing. {#b:5aa2b729}

**2. Earnest Money.** Buyer has deposited **$15,000.00** of earnest money with the Escrow Agent, receipt acknowledged. {#b:aa80c4ed}

**3. Title and Survey.** {#b:cc8ed2e9}
- 3.1 Seller shall furnish a Title Commitment from Lone Mesa Title Company. {#b:fe5dbec3}
- 3.2 Buyer, at Buyer's expense, may obtain a new boundary survey. {#b:1492da3e}
- 3.3 Buyer may object in writing to matters disclosed by the Commitment or survey that render title unmarketable or that prevent the intended use. Seller shall have a stated period to cure; if Seller cannot or does not cure, Buyer may terminate and receive a refund of the earnest money, or waive the objection and close. {#b:99bc700f}

**4. Property Condition / Feasibility.** Buyer has a feasibility period during which Buyer may terminate for any reason and receive a refund of the earnest money, time being of the essence. {#b:32db5897}

**5. Closing.** Closing shall occur on or before **February 27, 2026**, unless extended by written amendment. {#b:6d89bcc0}

**6. Conveyance.** At closing Seller shall deliver a deed conveying good and indefeasible title, subject only to the permitted exceptions. *[The form's default is a general warranty deed; the type of deed and the permitted exceptions are left to be finalized — this is one of the open negotiation terms.]* {#b:ba4e5329}

**7. Reservations.** *The contract as signed contains no reservation of any mineral interest by Seller and no reservation of any surface easement, water well, or access right in favor of Seller.* {#b:059528e2}

**8. Access.** The Property is represented to have legal access. *[The parties later learned the recorded access easement does not match the road on the ground — see Exhibit m14.exh.002 and m14.exh.003.]* {#b:eef8f21c}

**9. Brokers.** Buyer is assisted by a licensed real-estate agent; Seller listed the tract with a ranch brokerage. Commissions are as separately agreed. {#b:5afa9430}

Signed: **Garrett Whitfield** (Seller) · **Kenji Nakamura** (Buyer) · November 14, 2025. {#b:277e23d8}
