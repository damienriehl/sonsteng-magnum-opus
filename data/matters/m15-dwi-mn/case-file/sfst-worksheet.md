- **Location:** Right shoulder, eastbound Highway 10

## Horizontal Gaze Nystagmus (HGN)

- Lack of smooth pursuit: both eyes noted
- Distinct nystagmus at maximum deviation: both eyes noted
- Onset prior to 45 degrees: noted
- Deputy notation: subject squinting and eyes watering in wind

## Walk-and-Turn

- Clues recorded: starts before instructions complete; steps off line; improper turn
- Deputy notation: subject stated the ground was "solid ice"; performed on packed snow surface

## One-Leg-Stand

- Clues recorded: sways while balancing; puts foot down (twice)
- Deputy notation: subject stated he was cold and tired; visible shivering

## Officer Summary

Multiple clues observed across the battery. Deputy formed the opinion that the subject was impaired. Preliminary breath test administered thereafter; arrest followed.

*Note of record: tests were performed on a non-level, ice-packed shoulder in extreme cold and wind; subject reported cold, fatigue, and asked to relocate. Instrument-based confirmation followed at the jail.*
