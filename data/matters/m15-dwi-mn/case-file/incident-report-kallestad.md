## Narrative {#b:2a3410c8}

On the above date at approximately 22:52 hours I received a dispatch advising of a citizen report of a dark full-size pickup weaving eastbound on Highway 10. The reporting party gave approximate mile markers. I positioned on Highway 10 and located a dark pickup matching the description traveling eastbound. I fell in behind the vehicle and followed it for approximately one and one-half miles. During that time I observed the vehicle touch the painted fog line on the right side of the lane on one occasion. I initiated a traffic stop at 22:58 and the vehicle pulled onto the right shoulder. {#b:5eac7813}

I made contact with the driver, identified by license as Travis O. Wenzloff. Upon speaking with him I detected an odor of an alcoholic beverage. I observed that his eyes were glassy and watery and that he fumbled while retrieving his driver's license from his wallet. When asked, Wenzloff stated he had come from the Longspur Tap House and had "a couple beers." {#b:9ca7dee5}

I asked Wenzloff to step out to perform standardized field sobriety tests. Conditions on the shoulder were extreme cold with wind, and the surface was snow- and ice-packed. Wenzloff stated he was cold and asked whether the tests could be done elsewhere; the tests were completed at the location of the stop. I administered the horizontal-gaze-nystagmus test, the walk-and-turn, and the one-leg-stand. Wenzloff displayed multiple clues across the tests (see SFST worksheet, filed separately). Based on the totality of my observations I formed the opinion that Wenzloff was impaired. {#b:78e07560}

I administered a preliminary breath test at the roadside and placed Wenzloff under arrest. He was transported to the Sherburne County Jail. The evidentiary breath test was administered by the certified DataMaster operator on duty; the reported result was 0.09. Wenzloff was read the Implied Consent Advisory and served notice of license revocation. His vehicle was towed. He was booked and released the following morning. {#b:6b662215}

The stop and the field sobriety tests are recorded on my squad dashcam and body-worn camera. {#b:5ff32f25}

**Deputy Reid Kallestad, SCSO #214** {#b:5bab62f5}
