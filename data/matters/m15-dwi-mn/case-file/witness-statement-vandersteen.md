# WITNESS STATEMENT — Corinne Vandersteen

**Witness m15.wit.vandersteen**

- **Name:** Corinne Vandersteen
- **Role:** Motorist who called 911 to report a weaving vehicle
- **Statement taken:** February 4, 2025

My name is Corinne Vandersteen. On the night of January 17th I was driving home eastbound on Highway 10 a little before eleven. It was bitter cold and the roads had that hard-pack shine to them where they'd been plowed but not really cleared.

There was a dark pickup a ways ahead of me — I want to say two or three cars up, so I didn't have a clean look at it the whole time. It was drifting inside its lane, kind of wandering side to side, and then I saw it cross over the fog line on the right, the white line, and come back. That's what made me nervous. I've got kids and I don't mess around with that, so I called it in. I told the dispatcher there was a pickup weaving and gave them the mile markers as best I could.

I want to be fair about this. I never saw the truck swerve into oncoming traffic or almost hit anybody. It was more of a drift than anything dramatic. And by the time the squad got behind a truck, there were other vehicles on the road and I'd fallen back, so honestly I can't swear the truck they pulled over was the exact same one I'd been watching. It looked like the same kind of pickup, dark and full-size, but I wasn't right on top of it. I lost track of it for a bit around the interchange.

I didn't stop or talk to the deputy that night; I just kept driving once I saw the lights come on behind a truck. I gave my name to the dispatcher and they called me later.

That's what I remember. I'm happy to help however I can, but I don't want to say I'm sure about something I'm not sure about.

**Corinne Vandersteen**
