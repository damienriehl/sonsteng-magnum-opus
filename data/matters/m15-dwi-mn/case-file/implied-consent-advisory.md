## Advisory Read to Subject

The subject was advised, at the Sherburne County Jail, of the following in substance:

- That Minnesota law requires a person to take a test to determine whether the person is under the influence of alcohol.
- That refusal to take a test is a crime.
- That the subject had the right to consult with an attorney before testing, that this right is limited to the extent that it cannot unreasonably delay administration of the test, and that a telephone and directories would be made available.

The subject was afforded an opportunity to contact an attorney. Time telephone made available: 23:19. The subject did not complete a call to counsel before testing. Test offered and taken: evidentiary breath test.

## Notice of Revocation

Based on a reported alcohol concentration of 0.09, the subject was served with a Notice and Order of Revocation of driving privileges. The subject was advised of the right to seek judicial review of the revocation within the time allowed by law.

## Commercial Driving Privileges

The subject holds a Class A commercial driver's license. The subject was advised that a qualifying impaired-driving incident carries consequences for commercial driving privileges as provided by law.

*Prior-record notation: subject's driving record reflects a prior impaired-driving incident. See DVS abstract (filed separately).*
