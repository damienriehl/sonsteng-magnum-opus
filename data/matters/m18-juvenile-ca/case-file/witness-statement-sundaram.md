# Witness Statement — Priyanka Sundaram, Teacher

**Witness m18.wit.sundaram**

*Statement given to Alta Mesa County Probation intake, April 22, 2026. Recorded and summarized in the witness's own words.*

---

My name is Priyanka Sundaram. I teach chemistry at Encinal Ridge High School. I've been there six years.

I was at the game Friday night and left around a quarter to nine. My car was fine when I left — I would have noticed. I got the call Saturday that my car had been vandalized in the lot. When I went in to see it, there was spray paint on the passenger side and a long scratch keyed all the way down the driver's side, front fender to the back door. The scratch is the worse part. The estimate to repaint that whole side and fix it came to over thirteen hundred dollars.

I did not see who did it. I wasn't there when it happened. But I'll say this: I've had a difficult student this year, Andre Sotelo. He was failing my class and we'd had some words about it, and about a grade he wanted changed. I'm not accusing anyone of anything I didn't see. I'm just telling you there was tension there, and it crossed my mind when I saw my car.

I want to be made whole for the damage. That car is how I get to work. Whoever is responsible, or their families, I think should have to pay for the repair. I understand these are kids and I'm not trying to ruin anyone's life, but somebody keyed my car on purpose and I'm out the money until it's fixed.

*Statement of Priyanka Sundaram.*
