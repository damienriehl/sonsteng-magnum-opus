**Re: In re J.C., a Minor — Petition No. JV-2026-01147 — Informal Diversion Offer**

Dear Counsel:

Following review of the above matter, and considering that the minor has no prior juvenile record and is a first-time referral, this office is prepared to offer an informal diversion resolution in lieu of proceeding on the pending wardship petition. The proposed terms are as follows:

1. **Community service** — forty (40) hours, to be completed within ninety days.
2. **Accountability program** — completion of the county's youth accountability and decision-making class.
3. **Restitution** — payment of the minor's apportioned share of documented property-damage restitution to the affected vehicle owners and the school district.
4. **School and conduct conditions** — maintained enrollment, attendance, and no further law-enforcement contact during the diversion period.

Successful completion of all terms within the diversion period will result in the petition being dismissed with no adjudication and no wardship. Diversion of this kind ordinarily contemplates the minor's acknowledgment of responsibility for the underlying conduct as part of the accountability component.

If the offer is declined, or if the minor does not complete the program, the matter will proceed on the petition to a contested jurisdictional hearing.

**This offer will remain open until June 19, 2026.** Please advise this office of the minor's decision on or before that date.

Very truly yours,

*/s/ Renata Villalobos*
Deputy District Attorney, Juvenile Unit
