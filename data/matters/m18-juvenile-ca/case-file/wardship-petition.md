The People of the State of California, by the Alta Mesa County District Attorney, allege: {#b:6af56b93}

1. The above-named minor, J.C., is a female child of the age of sixteen years, residing within the County of Alta Mesa. {#b:3ef0515b}

2. The minor comes within the description of Welfare and Institutions Code section 602 in that, on or about April 10, 2026, in the County of Alta Mesa, the minor participated in an act that, if committed by an adult, would constitute the crime of vandalism, in that school property and private motor vehicles located in the parking lot of Encinal Ridge High School were defaced and damaged. {#b:d22be023}

3. The amount of the damage alleged is greater than four hundred dollars ($400). {#b:67f5c775}

4. The minor is in need of the care, treatment, and guidance of the Juvenile Court. {#b:f9439f59}

WHEREFORE, the People pray that the minor be adjudged a ward of the Court and that the Court make such orders as may be appropriate. {#b:8a716474}
