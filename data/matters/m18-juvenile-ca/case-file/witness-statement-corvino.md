# Witness Statement — Gerald Corvino, Assistant Principal {#b:df29cf2f}

**Witness m18.wit.corvino** {#b:81f70056}

*Statement given to Alta Mesa County Probation intake, April 21, 2026. Recorded and summarized in the witness's own words.* {#b:c61d2a43}

---

My name is Gerald Corvino. I am an assistant principal at Encinal Ridge High School and I have worked at the school for eleven years. Part of my job is student discipline and campus safety. {#b:e1ebeb27}

On Saturday morning, April 11, our head custodian called me at home to say the north parking lot and the gym wall had been vandalized overnight — paint on the wall and on some cars, and one car scratched up pretty badly. We had a home basketball game Friday night, so there were a lot of people coming and going until maybe nine. {#b:3d9ff52f}

On Monday I pulled the security footage with our resource officer. The video is not great — it's dark and grainy — but I could make out three kids. I recognized Jasmine Cordero right away. I've dealt with Jasmine before, so I know her. I identified the other two as Kayla Trejo and Andre Sotelo. {#b:5ccac4e7}

I called Jasmine down to my office Monday to ask her about it. I want to be clear that I asked her first because she was the one I recognized first on the video, not for any other reason. Deputy Haddad stepped in for part of the conversation. Jasmine told me she'd been in the lot and had her phone out, but she said she didn't spray anything. Her mother came in later that day. {#b:a227028f}

I'll be honest that Jasmine and I have had friction. She filed a complaint about me back in February over a dress-code call. I stand by how I handled that, but I know she feels I have it out for her. I don't. I recognized her on the tape, and that's why her name came up first. On the video, the one I believe is Jasmine is standing back holding something up — it looks like she's recording — while the other two are up at the wall and the cars. {#b:3a7733f4}

That's what I know. {#b:6418a0d6}

*Statement of Gerald Corvino.* {#b:e486ff84}
