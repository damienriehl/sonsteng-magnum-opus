# Instructor Notes — m02 (Winterhalt Discipline)

*Instructor-only. Not for student distribution. No answer key; teaching pointers only. Citations below are to the Meridian canon and are instructor-verified at publish against `data/jurisdictions/meridian.json`.*

## Shape and teaching goals

Attorney-discipline / professional-responsibility exercise, authored for the **respondent's counsel** side. The pedagogical payload is threefold: (1) the trust-accounting duties and the sanction-defining line between commingling/recordkeeping and misappropriation; (2) a hard client interview where the most consequential facts are gated behind rapport and confidentiality reassurance; and (3) the student's own candor duty as respondent's counsel.

## Governing Meridian authorities (instructor reference)

- **Safekeeping of client property / trust accounting:** Mer. R. Prof. Conduct 1.15 (commingling, intact preservation, prompt delivery, accounting, required books and records).
- **Diligence and communication:** Mer. R. Prof. Conduct 1.3 and 1.4 (the Osei non-communication count).
- **Bar cooperation / candor in the disciplinary process:** Mer. R. Prof. Conduct 8.1(b); candor to the tribunal, Mer. R. Prof. Conduct 3.3; the no-contact rule, Mer. R. Prof. Conduct 4.2 (the Marchetti interview trap).
- **Process and forum:** Meridian Office of Lawyer Conduct (MOLC) prosecutes; LPRB hearing panel / referee takes evidence; the **Meridian Supreme Court** imposes the sanction and holds original jurisdiction over discipline (see `meridian.json` → `attorney_discipline`). Referee here: Hon. Ingrid Solvang.
- **Legal-malpractice SOL** (context only, not charged): Mer. Stat. § 210.18 (4 years).

## The characterization pivot (discuss explicitly)

The whole case turns on whether the temporary shortfall is **negligent commingling + recordkeeping failure** (typically reprimand/probation, sometimes short suspension) or **misappropriation** — knowing use of client funds for the lawyer's own purposes (presumptively suspension or disbarment in most jurisdictions). Fact 012 (the ~$3,100 used for rent/payroll) is the concealed fact that pushes toward misappropriation. Strong students earn it in the interview and then counsel the client candidly rather than burying it.

## Interview design (persona engine)

- **Winterhalt** (distressed): volunteers mitigation, admits the commingling if asked, but gates the mail-avoidance (fact 023; wellbeing + acknowledged emotion), the drinking/recovery (fact 024; confidentiality reassurance + nonjudgmental response), and conceals the borrowed funds (fact 012). Watch for students who bulldoze and never earn the gated material.
- **Marchetti** (represented — Rule 4.2 teaching moment): the app should surface the no-contact question. Reward students who route through Beckwith; flag those who barrel ahead.
- **Fenwick** (cooperative, unrepresented): corroborates the software failure and, on rapport, the drinking observation (fact 024).
