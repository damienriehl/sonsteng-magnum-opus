# Exhibit m07.exh.007 — Independent Refrigeration Engineer Inspection Report {#b:cb62d3d7}

**Prepared by:** Ingemar Rask, P.E., consulting refrigeration engineer
**Retained by:** Cloverdyke Dairy Cooperative
**Inspection date:** February 27, 2026
**Report date:** March 3, 2026 {#b:9b7497e3}

*Reproduced summary of the engineer's written inspection report. Observations and findings only.* {#b:82beecb8}

---

**Scope.** Inspect the installed bulk milk-cooling unit at the Cloverdyke receiving station; review the operator temperature log and the servicer's reports; form an independent opinion on the cause of the repeated temperature failures. {#b:3a2a88d7}

**Observations — refrigeration system.** {#b:5c9cfc01}
1. The condensing unit's refrigerant charge and expansion-valve setting were found outside the correct range for this equipment and application, indicating the system was not properly charged and adjusted at installation. {#b:18a44ccd}
2. Sizing analysis against the co-op's peak incoming volume shows the unit as configured to be marginally undersized for peak load, with little capacity margin. {#b:70b1dab7}

**Observations — electrical supply.** {#b:7e132151}
3. A voltage imbalance was measured on one leg of the milk-house three-phase panel. Voltage imbalance stresses a compressor and can contribute to nuisance trips. {#b:01d9f8b4}

**Observations — maintenance.** {#b:1f617d97}
4. Condenser coils showed evidence of prior fouling. Fouled coils raise head pressure and degrade cooling; the servicer cleaned the coils on the December visit. {#b:85b64da5}

**Findings on causation.** Causation is mixed. The report identifies (a) an installation/configuration defect in the unit, (b) inadequate capacity margin for the operation, and (c) an electrical-supply imbalance on the building side, with (d) intermittent coil fouling as an aggravating factor. The report states that a unit charged, adjusted, and sized as this one was found would operate without a safety margin even under good maintenance, and that the observed pattern of repeated failures is consistent with such a system stressed by both configuration and power problems. {#b:97de1df3}

*This exhibit is offered as the co-op's independent engineering assessment. It supports both a defect theory and a mixed-causation picture that the opposing party will use.* {#b:408216cb}
