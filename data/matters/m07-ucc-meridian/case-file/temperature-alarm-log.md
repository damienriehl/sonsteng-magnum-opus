# Exhibit m07.exh.003 — Milk-House Temperature and Alarm Log (Excerpts)

**Keeper:** Reuben Tuvang, milk-house operator
**Source:** Handwritten milk-house shift log, Cloverdyke Dairy Cooperative
**Excerpt range:** November 2025 – December 2025

*The following are transcribed excerpts of the operator's handwritten log for the three failure dates. Times are approximate as recorded on the floor.*

---

**Nov 3, 2025 — a.m. shift (Tuvang).** "Came in ~5:40. HIGH TEMP ALARM sounding. Tank warm to the touch. My therm reads ~55°F. Compressor not holding. Milk in tank spoiled — approx 6,200 lb. Called Nils. Nils calling Brandt. Dumped load per Larkspur, ticket to follow."

**Nov 24, 2025 — a.m. shift (Tuvang).** "Tank temp climbing when I checked mid-shift. Compressor found OFF / locked out. No early alarm heard. By discovery load already warm — approx 5,800 lb affected. Larkspur will not accept. Brandt tech (Darius) out same day, replaced a contactor."

**Dec 12–14, 2025 — weekend (entry made Mon a.m., Tuvang).** "Weekend — no continuous floor coverage. Temp drifted up over Sat/Sun. Monday reading well above spec. Approx 7,000 lb downgraded, then dumped — Larkspur reject. Brandt tech out, cleaned condenser coils (fouled), reset controls."

**Operator note (undated, end of December).** "Ran short two people in Nov. Scheduled condenser cleaning slipped that month. Coils were dirtier than the schedule allows by the time Darius got to them in Dec. Noting for the file."

---

*This exhibit is offered as the contemporaneous operator record of the three temperature failures. The operator's handwritten log is kept separately from the unit's internal controller, whose recoverable data is presently unknown.*
