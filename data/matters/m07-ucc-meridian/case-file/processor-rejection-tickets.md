# Exhibit m07.exh.005 — Larkspur Creamery Rejection Tickets and Credit Memos

**Processor:** Larkspur Creamery
**Grader:** Corinne Callahan
**Supplier:** Cloverdyke Dairy Cooperative

*Transcribed summary of the three rejection tickets and associated credit memos in the co-op's file.*

---

**Rejection Ticket A — November 3, 2025.**
Load: bulk milk, Cloverdyke.
Reason: "Received warm / out of temperature. Bacteria screen fail. REJECTED — dumped."
Quantity affected: approx. 6,200 lb.
Associated credit memo: quality deduction and non-payment for rejected load.

**Rejection Ticket B — November 24, 2025.**
Load: bulk milk, Cloverdyke.
Reason: "Received warm. REJECTED — dumped."
Quantity affected: approx. 5,800 lb.
Associated credit memo: quality deduction and non-payment for rejected load.

**Rejection Ticket C — December 15, 2025 (weekend load).**
Load: bulk milk, Cloverdyke.
Reason: "Downgraded then rejected — temperature abuse over weekend. Dumped."
Quantity affected: approx. 7,000 lb.
Associated credit memo: quality deduction and non-payment for rejected load.

---

**Summary of loss reflected:** Approximately 19,000 pounds of milk rejected across the three events, plus loss of the quality premium on the affected loads. These figures form part of the co-op's approximately $28,000 consequential-loss calculation, which also includes emergency repairs and the rented portable cooling trailer.

*This exhibit is offered as the third-party processor's documentation of the rejected loads. Grader Callahan notes the co-op otherwise runs a clean operation and attributes these three rejections to warm milk on arrival.*
