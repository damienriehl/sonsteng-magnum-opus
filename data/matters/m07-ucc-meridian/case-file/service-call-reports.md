# Exhibit m07.exh.004 — Brandt Field Service Call Reports (Three Visits) {#b:4517fc01}

**Servicer:** Brandt Refrigeration Systems, LLC
**Technician:** Darius Pelletier
**Customer:** Cloverdyke Dairy Cooperative {#b:77bc6460}

*Transcribed from the three Brandt service tickets in the co-op's file.* {#b:ad4f7a4e}

---

**Service Ticket 1 — November 3, 2025.**
Complaint: "Unit lost temperature overnight; tank warm; product loss reported by customer."
Findings/Work: "Compressor locked out on arrival. Reset controls. Metered incoming power — possible incoming power irregularity noted on one leg; advise customer have electrician verify service. Unit running on departure."
Tech: D. Pelletier. {#b:fc2dc055}

**Service Ticket 2 — November 24, 2025.**
Complaint: "Compressor off; second temperature failure; product loss reported."
Findings/Work: "Found failed contactor. Replaced contactor. Verified operation. Recommend customer keep condenser maintenance current per Schedule B; coils beginning to foul."
Tech: D. Pelletier. {#b:15b57ca1}

**Service Ticket 3 — December 12, 2025.**
Complaint: "Temperature drift over weekend; third failure; product downgraded."
Findings/Work: "Condenser coils fouled — cleaned coils. Reset control board. Head pressure high on arrival consistent with fouled coils. Reiterated power-supply concern from prior visit. Recommend electrical service check and adherence to maintenance schedule."
Tech: D. Pelletier. {#b:3e15473f}

---

*This exhibit is offered as the servicer's contemporaneous account of each visit. It reflects Brandt's two recurring themes — a suspected power-supply irregularity and fouled condenser coils — as well as the specific repairs performed.* {#b:bcdf8cca}
