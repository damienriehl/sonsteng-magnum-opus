# Exhibit m07.exh.001 — Equipment Purchase Agreement and Limited Warranty {#b:41e9311f}

**Parties:** Cloverdyke Dairy Cooperative ("Buyer") and Brandt Refrigeration Systems, LLC ("Seller")
**Agreement date:** September 10, 2025
**Subject:** One bulk milk-cooling and refrigeration unit, installed
**Total price:** $96,000.00 {#b:b683d3f8}

*Reproduced below are the operative provisions of the signed agreement. Exhibit is offered as a true copy of the document in the co-op's file.* {#b:ffeddfed}

---

**1. Equipment and Price.** Seller shall furnish and install at Buyer's receiving station near Danforth one (1) bulk milk-cooling and refrigeration unit, together with the controls and condensing equipment described in the attached specification sheet, for the total price of Ninety-Six Thousand and 00/100 Dollars ($96,000.00), payable per the schedule in Section 2. {#b:26abbf00}

**3. Performance Specification.** Seller warrants that, when installed and operated in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions and the maintenance schedule attached as Schedule B, the Unit shall maintain the bulk tank contents at or below forty degrees Fahrenheit (40°F) and shall reduce fresh incoming milk to thirty-eight degrees Fahrenheit (38°F) within the pull-down time stated in the specification sheet. {#b:68a5cfaf}

**7. Limited Warranty.** Seller warrants the Unit to be free from defects in material and workmanship for a period of twenty-four (24) months from the date of installation. Seller's obligation under this warranty is expressly limited to the repair or replacement, at Seller's option, of any part found by Seller to be defective. This is the Buyer's sole and exclusive remedy. {#b:5092428d}

**8. Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** IN NO EVENT SHALL SELLER BE LIABLE FOR INCIDENTAL OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES OF ANY KIND, INCLUDING WITHOUT LIMITATION LOST OR SPOILED PRODUCT, LOST PRODUCTION, LOST PROFITS, OR THE COST OF SUBSTITUTE COOLING. Buyer's remedies are limited to those stated in Section 7. {#b:ffe446d4}

**9. Buyer Maintenance Obligations.** Buyer shall maintain the Unit in accordance with Schedule B, including cleaning of the condenser coils not less than quarterly, and shall keep the Unit registered with Seller. Failure to perform required maintenance may void warranty coverage as to defects caused by such failure. {#b:5866d8f0}

**11. Dispute Resolution.** The parties shall first submit any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement to non-binding mediation. Any dispute not resolved in mediation shall be submitted to binding arbitration under the rules then in effect for commercial disputes, seated in Meridian. {#b:c0376de7}

*Signature block:* For Buyer, Nils Aaberg, General Manager, Cloverdyke Dairy Cooperative. For Seller, Sylvia Brandt, Operations Manager, Brandt Refrigeration Systems, LLC. Dated September 10, 2025. {#b:75ea3639}
