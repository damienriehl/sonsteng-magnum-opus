# Exhibit m07.exh.008 — Ostrowski Electrical Service Note (Pre-Purchase Panel Warning) {#b:257304f7}

**Electrician:** Dale Ostrowski (independent electrical contractor)
**Customer:** Cloverdyke Dairy Cooperative
**Service note date:** April 2025 (several months before the cooling-unit purchase) {#b:b1366f24}

*Transcribed from a service invoice/note found in the co-op's maintenance file. This document is internal to the co-op and was not part of the Brandt transaction.* {#b:c392df34}

---

**Service note.** "Responded to intermittent breaker complaint at receiving station. Checked main and subpanels. Advised customer that the existing three-phase panel serving the milk-house is undersized and aging for the current and expected load, and shows signs of wear. Recommended a panel upgrade and a dedicated circuit for any new large refrigeration equipment. Customer to consider budget for upgrade. Temporary fix applied to the immediate breaker complaint." {#b:f023e020}

**Handwritten margin note (co-op file).** "Board reviewed — upgrade deferred this cycle to hold down costs. Revisit." {#b:a66b00f3}

---

*This exhibit is offered for its bearing on the electrical-supply issue and on the co-op's pre-purchase knowledge of a panel problem. It is sensitive: it shows the co-op was warned about the panel and deferred the upgrade. Counsel should weigh how and whether it surfaces, and be aware the general manager treats the pre-purchase warning as a delicate subject.* {#b:0619f19b}
