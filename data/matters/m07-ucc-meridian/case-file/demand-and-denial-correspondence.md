# Exhibit m07.exh.006 — Warranty-Claim Demand Letter and Brandt Denial

*This exhibit collects two letters: the co-op's warranty-claim demand and Brandt's response. Reproduced as true copies from the file.*

---

## Letter 1 — Cloverdyke Demand

**Date:** January 20, 2026
**From:** Nils Aaberg, General Manager, Cloverdyke Dairy Cooperative
**To:** Brandt Refrigeration Systems, LLC

Dear Ms. Brandt:

I am writing on behalf of Cloverdyke Dairy Cooperative regarding the bulk milk-cooling unit your company sold and installed for us on October 15, 2025. As you know, the unit failed to hold temperature three separate times in its first two months of service — on November 3, November 24, and over the weekend of December 12. Each failure spoiled a full load of milk. In total we lost roughly nineteen thousand pounds of milk, our quality premium on those loads, and we have paid out of pocket for emergency repairs and a rented portable cooling trailer to keep operating.

The unit has not performed as your agreement promised. We are asking that Brandt replace the unit with equipment that will actually hold our milk cold, and that Brandt make the co-op whole for our losses from these failures, which we currently calculate at approximately $28,000, in addition to the unit itself.

Please respond within fourteen days. We would prefer to resolve this cooperatively.

Sincerely,
Nils Aaberg

---

## Letter 2 — Brandt Response

**Date:** February 5, 2026
**From:** Brandt Refrigeration Systems, LLC
**To:** Nils Aaberg, Cloverdyke Dairy Cooperative

Dear Mr. Aaberg:

We received your letter. We take the performance of our equipment seriously, and our technician responded promptly to each of your service calls.

Our review indicates that the failures were caused by conditions outside our equipment — specifically, an irregularity in the electrical service at your facility and a lapse in the required condenser maintenance, both of which our service reports document. Under our written warranty, our obligation is limited to the repair or replacement of defective parts, and the agreement expressly excludes consequential damages such as spoiled product and substitute cooling costs.

Accordingly, we decline your request for payment of the claimed losses. We remain willing to re-inspect the unit and to repair any part we determine to be defective, consistent with the warranty. Please contact our office to arrange an inspection.

Regards,
Brandt Refrigeration Systems, LLC
