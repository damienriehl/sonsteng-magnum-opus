Thank you for retaining Ellingboe & Ravndal LLP. This letter sets out the terms of our engagement. {#b:1ebef803}

**Scope.** We will represent Cloverdyke Dairy Cooperative in connection with its dispute against Brandt Refrigeration Systems, LLC arising from the bulk milk-cooling unit the co-op purchased on September 10, 2025 and the unit's repeated failures to hold temperature. Our work will include investigating and developing the facts, analyzing the co-op's warranty claims and available remedies, pursuing recovery through the demand, mediation, and, if necessary, the binding arbitration contemplated by your purchase agreement. This engagement does not include any separate litigation, appeal, or unrelated matter, which would be the subject of a further agreement. {#b:e48a68ff}

**Fees.** This is an hourly engagement. Amara Ellingboe (senior attorney) bills at $250 per hour and Marcus Ravndal (associate attorney) bills at $225 per hour. Paralegal time, if used, bills at $120 per hour. We bill in one-tenth-hour increments and send monthly statements. {#b:6d265d05}

**Costs.** You are responsible for out-of-pocket costs we advance on your behalf — for example, the independent engineer's fees, any mediation or arbitration filing and neutral fees, and copying and delivery charges. These appear as expenses on your statements. {#b:2eb50ed8}

**Billing and payment.** Statements are due within thirty days. We understand the co-op is a member-owned operation watching its costs, and we are glad to discuss the pace of work and budget at any point. {#b:96861d0b}

**Your role.** Please keep us promptly informed, preserve all documents and records relating to the unit and the milk losses, and route any communications with Brandt or its representatives through us. Because Brandt is represented by counsel, neither you nor we may contact Brandt's people directly about this dispute. {#b:3c34ab20}

**Termination.** You may end this engagement at any time; we may withdraw as permitted by the rules of professional conduct. On termination you remain responsible for fees and costs incurred through that date. {#b:0c57c0e6}

If these terms are acceptable, please sign below and return a copy. We are glad to be working with the co-op. {#b:f669234e}
