# Exhibit m04.exh.003 — Plat Map and Surveyor's Diagram (Described) {#b:305435be}

*Described exhibit. The 1971 "Kestrel Point Shores" plat overlaid with Nadia Brummond's March 2026 survey. Accompanies the Surveyor's Report (see witness-brummond.md).* {#b:4c5f388d}

**The 1971 plat (as depicted):** {#b:5ea7c901}

- The subdivision "Kestrel Point Shores" is drawn as a row of lakefront lots along the north shore of Lake Verdant. Lots are numbered; 4400 and 4380 Kestrel Point are adjacent, with 4380 lying between 4400 and the county road. {#b:15ed5119}
- A dedicated but unbuilt shoreline road, **Verdant Shore Road**, is drawn running along the rear (lake side) of the lots, including behind 4400. On the ground it does not exist. {#b:97e1d25b}
- No driveway or access lane to 4400 is shown on the plat itself; the only depicted public access is the county road at the north edge of the subdivision, which does not touch 4400. {#b:87cee208}
