# Exhibit m13.exh.006 — Property-Damage Estimate and Total-Loss Valuation {#b:83e6163a}

**Vehicle:** 2019 four-door sedan
**Owner:** Sofia Reyes
**Assessed by:** Claimant's auto insurer, property-damage unit {#b:a95c2c9c}

## Damage Description {#b:c383bb6d}
Impact to the right (passenger) side: crushed right front fender, right front and rear passenger doors, deformed B-pillar, and frame intrusion into the passenger compartment. Both frontal airbags deployed. Right front wheel and suspension displaced. Vehicle rotated approximately 90 degrees on impact. {#b:e0227bdf}

## Repair Estimate {#b:25868178}
Estimated repair cost: **approximately $9,800**, including structural, body, airbag-module, and mechanical repairs. {#b:3d1a69b0}

## Valuation and Disposition {#b:25cf7169}
Pre-loss actual cash value of the vehicle was assessed at approximately $8,600. Because the estimated repair cost exceeded the vehicle's value, the insurer **declared the vehicle a total loss**. The claimant owned the vehicle free of any lien. {#b:7da79fda}
