# WITNESS STATEMENT — Margo Selkirk

**Witness m05.wit.selkirk**

- **Name:** Margo Selkirk
- **Role:** Sous chef, The Copper Larder; the birthday celebrant
- **Statement taken:** March 12, 2026

My name is Margo Selkirk and I'm the sous chef at The Copper Larder on Wexford Avenue. February seventh was my birthday, and after we closed the dining room a bunch of us stayed for a little dinner in the back. It was maybe eight or nine of us. Devon Halvard was there. He's one of our line cooks and he's a good guy, always the one who stays late to clean the station.

Devon came off the line a little after nine and sat down with us. I remember he had a couple of draft beers over the night while we ate and talked — I want to say two, because I was watching the beers come out and Trina was keeping the tab. When the cake came out I made everybody do a birthday toast, so there was a round of little whiskey shots, and Devon did the toast with us. After that it was mostly water and picking at the food. Nobody was hammered. It was a work birthday, not a rager.

Devon ate a real dinner, too. He had the staff meal and then he was grabbing off the shared plates like everybody. He'd worked a double that day — I know because I made the schedule — so honestly by the end he looked more tired than anything. He was yawning, rubbing his eyes.

When he left, maybe around ten thirty, he seemed fine to me. He wasn't stumbling, he wasn't slurring, he said goodnight and thanked me for the invite and headed out to his car. I was surprised when I heard he got arrested. That's not the Devon I saw walk out.

I'm willing to say all this in court if it helps him.

**Margo Selkirk**
