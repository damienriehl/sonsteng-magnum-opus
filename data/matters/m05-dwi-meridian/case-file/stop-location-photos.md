# STOP-LOCATION PHOTOGRAPHS AND SLOPE MEASUREMENT (DESCRIBED) {#b:3420606f}

**Exhibit m05.exh.009** {#b:b660b2e6}

- **Location:** Right shoulder, 800 block of Wexford Avenue, Norsholm {#b:3c479b73}
- **Photographs taken:** February 21, 2026 (daytime, for the defense) {#b:f1c91393}
- **Also referenced:** Officer's body-camera footage (Exhibit m05.exh.005) {#b:279fddfb}

## Description of the images {#b:64271f1c}

The photographs document the shoulder where the field sobriety tests were administered. They show a paved shoulder that grades noticeably downward from the traveled lane toward a storm drain at the edge of the pavement. A measurement taken at the same location recorded the cross-slope at roughly seven percent — a distinct downhill pitch rather than level ground. The photographs taken closer to the date of the stop, and the body-camera footage from the night itself, show snow and ice covering the shoulder surface. {#b:3e8e01e8}

## Points depicted {#b:28284858}

- The downward grade of the shoulder toward the storm drain. {#b:761a5a62}
- The proximity of the testing area to the drain and the curb line. {#b:66ed491d}
- The snow-packed and icy surface underfoot (from the night-of footage and the near-date photographs). {#b:53af6037}
- The absence of any flat, cleared area immediately adjacent where the tests could alternatively have been performed. {#b:60a270fc}

These images are offered to show the physical conditions under which balance-based field sobriety tests were conducted and are intended to be viewed alongside the officer's worksheet and the body-camera footage. {#b:8306a86e}
