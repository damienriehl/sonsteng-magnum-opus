# STOP-LOCATION PHOTOGRAPHS AND SLOPE MEASUREMENT (DESCRIBED)

**Exhibit m05.exh.009**

- **Location:** Right shoulder, 800 block of Wexford Avenue, Norsholm
- **Photographs taken:** February 21, 2026 (daytime, for the defense)
- **Also referenced:** Officer's body-camera footage (Exhibit m05.exh.005)

## Description of the images

The photographs document the shoulder where the field sobriety tests were administered. They show a paved shoulder that grades noticeably downward from the traveled lane toward a storm drain at the edge of the pavement. A measurement taken at the same location recorded the cross-slope at roughly seven percent — a distinct downhill pitch rather than level ground. The photographs taken closer to the date of the stop, and the body-camera footage from the night itself, show snow and ice covering the shoulder surface.

## Points depicted

- The downward grade of the shoulder toward the storm drain.
- The proximity of the testing area to the drain and the curb line.
- The snow-packed and icy surface underfoot (from the night-of footage and the near-date photographs).
- The absence of any flat, cleared area immediately adjacent where the tests could alternatively have been performed.

These images are offered to show the physical conditions under which balance-based field sobriety tests were conducted and are intended to be viewed alongside the officer's worksheet and the body-camera footage.
