## Narrative

On the above date and time I was on routine patrol traveling east on Wexford Avenue when I observed a sedan ahead of me with its rear passenger-side taillight not illuminated. I initiated a traffic stop for the equipment violation. The vehicle pulled to the right shoulder without incident.

I made contact with the driver, identified by license as Devon R. Halvard. Upon speaking with him I detected an odor of an alcoholic beverage coming from the vehicle. I observed that his eyes were bloodshot and watery and that his speech was slow. When I asked whether he had been drinking, Halvard stated that he had "had a couple beers" at a coworker's birthday.

I asked Halvard to step out of the vehicle to perform standardized field sobriety tests. I administered the horizontal-gaze-nystagmus test, the walk-and-turn, and the one-leg-stand on the shoulder adjacent to the driver's door. Halvard displayed multiple clues across the tests (see SFST worksheet, filed separately). Based on the totality of my observations I formed the opinion that Halvard was impaired. Halvard stated at one point that he would prefer to perform the tests somewhere flatter; the tests were completed at the location of the stop.

I administered a preliminary breath test at the roadside and placed Halvard under arrest. He was transported to the Norsholm Police Department. At 23:12 I read Halvard the Implied Consent Advisory. Halvard consented to an evidentiary breath test. The DMT-G instrument was operated by me; two samples were obtained, and the reported result was 0.08.

Halvard was booked and issued citations for Fourth-Degree DWI and the equipment violation. His vehicle was towed. He was released the following morning.

The stop, the field sobriety tests, and the subsequent contact are recorded on my squad dashcam and body-worn camera.
