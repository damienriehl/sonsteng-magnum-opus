## Narrative {#b:5141ec89}

On the above date and time I was on routine patrol traveling east on Wexford Avenue when I observed a sedan ahead of me with its rear passenger-side taillight not illuminated. I initiated a traffic stop for the equipment violation. The vehicle pulled to the right shoulder without incident. {#b:2b39b139}

I made contact with the driver, identified by license as Devon R. Halvard. Upon speaking with him I detected an odor of an alcoholic beverage coming from the vehicle. I observed that his eyes were bloodshot and watery and that his speech was slow. When I asked whether he had been drinking, Halvard stated that he had "had a couple beers" at a coworker's birthday. {#b:2049d10a}

I asked Halvard to step out of the vehicle to perform standardized field sobriety tests. I administered the horizontal-gaze-nystagmus test, the walk-and-turn, and the one-leg-stand on the shoulder adjacent to the driver's door. Halvard displayed multiple clues across the tests (see SFST worksheet, filed separately). Based on the totality of my observations I formed the opinion that Halvard was impaired. Halvard stated at one point that he would prefer to perform the tests somewhere flatter; the tests were completed at the location of the stop. {#b:588a9ae7}

I administered a preliminary breath test at the roadside and placed Halvard under arrest. He was transported to the Norsholm Police Department. At 23:12 I read Halvard the Implied Consent Advisory. Halvard consented to an evidentiary breath test. The DMT-G instrument was operated by me; two samples were obtained, and the reported result was 0.08. {#b:6d58679b}

Halvard was booked and issued citations for Fourth-Degree DWI and the equipment violation. His vehicle was towed. He was released the following morning. {#b:1c8b914f}

The stop, the field sobriety tests, and the subsequent contact are recorded on my squad dashcam and body-worn camera. {#b:757b1cbd}
