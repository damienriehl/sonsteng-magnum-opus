## What We're Building

A system that consolidates **Prof. John O. Sonsteng's life work** on legal-education reform
into a **layered "Magnum Opus"** — and turns it into a **living practicum** that trains the
next generation of advocates through a **human-and-AI ("centaur") apprenticeship**.

Three layers (Damien's decision):
1. **The Book** — a canonical treatise consolidating John's corpus (the intellectual core).
2. **The Curriculum** — his Skills Practicum, structured into teachable modules.
3. **The Platform** — AI-assisted delivery that scales 1:1 apprenticeship.

**Model:** Open source, **MIT-licensed / open-access** (extends the existing Open Resource Tool).
**Primary audience:** law students & newer lawyers first — extensible to all experience levels.

**First deliverable (today):** a single-page **narrative walkthrough website** Damien can walk
John & Roger through, to confirm direction before deeper work.

## Why This Approach

- **The story already forms a trilogy** we can unify into an opus:
  - *A Legal Education Renaissance: A Practical Approach for the 21st Century* (Sonsteng et al.,
    William Mitchell Law Review, ~2007) — **the diagnosis**.
  - **The Legal Practicum Method** + his live **General Practice: Skills Practicum** course
    (2023–2025 handbooks; original survey data on 17 legal-practice + 9 practice-management
    skills) — **the solution**.
  - **The Open Resource Tool** — open-access exercises run jointly by Mitchell Hamline's
    **C-LAB** and **IGUL at Bahçeşehir University (Istanbul)** — **the "Turkey" thread**, already
    open-spirited. Trialbook (3rd ed.) + the "Midstate" fictional jurisdiction are the substrate.
- **Why now:** AI finally makes NITA-style 1:1 apprenticeship *scalable* — unlimited reps + instant
  first-pass critique — without replacing human faculty.
- **Differentiation:** peers exist (Suffolk's AI mock judge, Stanford CodeX cross-exam simulator,
  AltaClaro DepoSim, StrongSuit) but **none marry a named scholar's life work + an open-source
  curriculum + a faculty-anchored centaur model.**

---

## The Centaur Model (core pedagogy)

Anchor = **human faculty critique** (the NITA tradition). AI **amplifies**, never replaces.

```
Student performs a rep (memo, cross, negotiation, argument)
        ↓
AI gives instant, rubric-based first-pass critique  →  unlimited reps, 24/7
        ↓
Human faculty coach judgment, ethics, strategy, the "why"  →  scarce time spent where it matters
        ↓
Mastery (measured against Sonsteng's skills framework)
```
