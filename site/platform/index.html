ABOUT THE METHOD

The centaur layer

Every exercise here ships with a machine-readable rubric and a revise-and-repeat loop — the two halves of a human+AI (“centaur”) pedagogy. AI is the default speaker and gives the first-pass assessment and feedback with unlimited reps; scarce faculty time is reserved for the high-value coaching only a human can give. The simulated client interviews, rubric critiques, and firm dashboard below are that layer, live.
