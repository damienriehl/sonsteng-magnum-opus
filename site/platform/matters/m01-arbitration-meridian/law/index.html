Canon note

Meridian is a wholly fictional U.S. state invented for the Legal Practicum corpus. Every name, court, statute number, reporter, judge, and place below is original and does not refer to any real jurisdiction, court, official, or person. All ten fictional-tier matters (m01-m10) are set here. Matter agents MUST draw courts, judges, citation formats, county/city names, and procedural framing from this file rather than improvising — this is the single source of Meridian truth. Government · Populous note

Norsholm, on the south shore of Lake Verdant, is both the capital and the largest city; the Kettleridge iron range anchors the north.

Government · Flavor

Upper-Midwest character: glacial prairie-pothole lakes country in the south and center, boreal pine forest and a played-out iron range in the north, row-crop and dairy agriculture through the river valleys. Scandinavian, German, and more recent Somali, Hmong, and Latino settlement. Long winters, ice-fishing, lake cabins, county fairs, and a strong tradition of civic volunteerism. Two principal rivers: the Vasser (running south through Norsholm) and the Kettle (draining the northern range). Time zone: Central.

Real estate conventions · Note

Recording, notary, and conveyancing conventions for the Meridian real-estate shape. Original to Meridian; functionally similar to an Upper-Midwest recording state with a dual abstract/Torrens system.

Real estate conventions · Recording office · Name

County Recorder

Real estate conventions · Recording office · Note

Deeds, mortgages, easements, and other instruments affecting title are recorded with the Recorder of the county where the land lies (e.g., 'Sable County Recorder'). Torrens (registered) land is handled by the County Registrar; the same office in most counties.

Real estate conventions · Recording office · Constructive notice

Proper recording gives constructive notice to subsequent purchasers; Meridian is a race-notice jurisdiction (Mer. Stat. § 507.34).

Real estate conventions · Title system

Dual system: most land is 'abstract' property (chain of title via recorded instruments and an abstract of title); some parcels are Torrens 'registered' land evidenced by a Certificate of Title.

Real estate conventions · Notary · Authority

Meridian Notary Public, commissioned by the Secretary of State under the Meridian Notary Act (Mer. Stat. ch. 358).

Real estate conventions · Notary · Note

A deed or mortgage must be acknowledged before a notary (or other authorized officer) to be recordable. Standard acknowledgment certificate and notary seal/stamp required.

Real estate conventions · Transfer taxes · Deed transfer tax

Real estate conventions · Transfer taxes · Mortgage registry tax

Real estate conventions · Common disputes

Easements, revocable licenses, boundary/curb-cut and access rights, and municipal authority over abutting roads and rights-of-way are recurring Meridian conveyancing issues (relevant to the real-estate shape).
