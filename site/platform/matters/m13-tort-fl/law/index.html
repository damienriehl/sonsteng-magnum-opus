Court structure · Trial courts · Circuit court · Name

Florida Circuit Court

Court structure · Trial courts · Circuit court · Note

Trial court of general jurisdiction, organized into 20 judicial circuits by county. Handles higher-value civil (incl. the m13 auto-negligence jury trial), felony criminal, family, and probate matters. Probate is heard in the Probate Division of the Circuit Court.

Court structure · Trial courts · County court · Name

Florida County Court

Court structure · Trial courts · County court · Note

Limited jurisdiction (lower-value civil, misdemeanors, traffic).

Court structure · Highest court · Note

Court structure · Specialty divisions · Probate

Probate Division of the Circuit Court — estate administration and will contests; captioned 'In re Estate of <Decedent>.'

Shape specific notes · Auto negligence

Florida negligence practice involves distinctive doctrines students should research (e.g., the state's comparative-fault approach and no-fault/PIP motor-vehicle insurance framework). Civil jury trial in the Circuit Court. Students research the current standard themselves.

Shape specific notes · Wills probate

Florida's constitutional and statutory HOMESTEAD protections sharply constrain how a homestead may be devised and shield it from creditors — the deliberate teaching hook for the m20 probate contest. Students research the current homestead-devise restrictions themselves.
