Real estate conventions · Note

The m14 shape is a Texas real-estate purchase negotiation. Instruments affecting title are recorded with the County Clerk of the county where the land lies; acknowledgment before a Texas notary public is required for recording. Texas is a notice jurisdiction. Students research the current conveyancing and title framework themselves.

Real estate conventions · Recording office

County Clerk (real property records)

Real estate conventions · Notary

Texas Notary Public, commissioned by the Secretary of State; acknowledgment required for recordable instruments.
