Court structure · Trial court · Name

Superior Court of California

Court structure · Trial court · Note

The single trial court of general jurisdiction, organized by county (e.g., 'Superior Court of California, County of ___'). Operates specialized divisions for juvenile and family matters.

Court structure · Trial court · Specialty divisions · Juvenile

Juvenile Division of the Superior Court — delinquency proceedings under Welfare & Institutions Code (petitions typically brought under WIC § 602). Records confidential; minors identified by initials.

Court structure · Trial court · Specialty divisions · Family

Family Law Division of the Superior Court — dissolution, custody, support, and community-property division.

Court structure · Intermediate appellate · Name

California Court of Appeal

Court structure · Intermediate appellate · Note

Intermediate appellate court, organized into six Appellate Districts.

Court structure · Highest court · Name

Supreme Court of California

Court structure · Highest court · Note

The court of last resort.

Shape specific notes · Juvenile

California juvenile delinquency proceedings run under the Welfare & Institutions Code (WIC), not the adult Penal Code process; dispositions, wardship, and sealing follow WIC. Cases captioned 'In re <initials>, a Minor.' Students research current WIC procedure themselves.

Shape specific notes · Dissolution

California is a COMMUNITY-PROPERTY state — property and debt acquired during marriage are presumptively community property divided equally at dissolution; this is the deliberate contrast the m19 shape teaches. Dissolution captioned 'In re the Marriage of <A> and <B>.' Students research the current Family Code framework themselves.

Shape specific notes · Noncompete crossref

California broadly voids employee non-compete covenants by statute — the contrast point referenced by the m16 (NY) shape.
