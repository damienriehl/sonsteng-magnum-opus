Court structure · Trial court · Name

Minnesota District Court

Court structure · Trial court · Note

Court of general jurisdiction, organized into ten judicial districts by county. Handles civil, criminal, family, juvenile, and probate matters.

Court structure · Trial court · Criminal note

Hears DWI/impaired-driving prosecutions.

Court structure · Intermediate appellate · Name

Minnesota Court of Appeals

Court structure · Intermediate appellate · Note

Intermediate appellate court; three-judge panels.

Court structure · Highest court · Name

Minnesota Supreme Court

Court structure · Highest court · Note

Court of last resort; also has original/ultimate jurisdiction over attorney discipline and admission.

Attorney discipline · Regulator · Name

Office of Lawyers Professional Responsibility

Attorney discipline · Regulator · Abbrev

OLPR

Attorney discipline · Regulator · Head title

Director of the Office of Lawyers Professional Responsibility

Attorney discipline · Regulator · Note

Investigates and prosecutes attorney-misconduct complaints; the Director files a Petition for Disciplinary Action. This is the real-tier analog of the fictional MOLC.

Attorney discipline · Board · Name

Lawyers Professional Responsibility Board

Attorney discipline · Board · Abbrev

LPRB

Attorney discipline · Board · Note

Board of lawyers and public members; district ethics committees screen complaints; a referee (often a retired judge) is appointed to hold the hearing and make findings.

Attorney discipline · Ultimate authority

Minnesota Supreme Court

Attorney discipline · Process flow

Complaint -> OLPR investigation -> district ethics committee -> Director's Petition for Disciplinary Action -> referee hearing -> Minnesota Supreme Court
